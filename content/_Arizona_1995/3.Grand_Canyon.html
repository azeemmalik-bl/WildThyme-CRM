Grand Canyon - Arizona
     
 
 

.

 

The first European known to have viewed the Grand Canyon was García López de Cárdenas from Spain, who arrived in 1540.

 
     
For thousands of years, the area has been continuously inhabited by Native Americans, who built settlements within the canyon and its many caves. The Pueblo people considered the Grand Canyon a holy site, and made pilgrimages to it.
The steep-sided canyon, the Grand Canyon, carved by the Colorado River, is 277 miles long, up to 18 miles wide and attains a depth of over a mile. Nearly two billion years of Earth's geological history have been exposed as the Colorado River and its tributaries cut their channels through layer after layer of rock while the Colorado Plateau was uplifted.

Up at 4.30am in order to see the sun rise over the Canyon; a truly magical experience. Then a full day walking around the Canyon and then seeing the sun finally set.
We'd booked ourselves into log cabins on the North Rim of the Canyon which was much less commercial than the South Rim: more difficult to reach and further away from Las Vegas. The cabins were fun and rustic. 
 
My camera was only a basic model and couldn't exactly capture the shades of colour apparent in the rock which formed the Canyon, so I have to include pictures from the postcards I bought to show off its true beauty.
