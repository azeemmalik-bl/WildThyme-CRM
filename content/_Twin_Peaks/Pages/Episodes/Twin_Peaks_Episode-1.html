1 - Traces To Nowhere

Written by Mark Frost and David Lynch

Directed by Duwayne Dunham

Original Airdate: 12 April 1990

Next →

“I carry a log - yes. Is it funny to you? It is not to me. Behind all things are reasons. Reasons can even explain the absurd. Do we have the time to learn the reasons behind the human being's varied behavior? I think not. Some take the time. Are they called detectives?

Watch - and see what life teaches.”

  • FBI Agent Dale Cooper and Sheriff Harry S. Truman discover more about the troubled secret life of the murdered Laura Palmer.
  • Big Ed Hurley reveals that he was drugged at the Roadhouse to knock him out.
  • A frightened James Hurley, vengeful Bobby Briggs and Mike Nelson are released from jail.
  • Catherine Martell and Benjamin Horne plot together to take over the Packard sawmill.
  • Mike and Bobby swear revenge on James Hurley.

Unanswered Questions

  • How long have Benjamin and Catherine been plotting together?
  • Does Josie suspect?
  • What action are they planning to take?
  • Who drugged Ed, and why? (See Rest In Pain).
