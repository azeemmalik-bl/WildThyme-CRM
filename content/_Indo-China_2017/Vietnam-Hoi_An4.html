Vietnam - Hoi An

This temple complex at My Son (pronounced Me Sun) was active as a religious centre between the 4th and 13th centuries (ie: the longest period of development of any monuments in Southeast Asia) was dedicated to the Cham kings associated with divinities such as Shiva. At one point in their history, the domes of some of the towers were covered with gold.

While the West wrings its hands over the destruction of Palmyra by ISIS, it's bitterly ironic that this complex was bombed out of existence by US troops in 1968 who were trying to flush out the Vietcong. Of the original 68 structures, only 20 now survive (most in poor shape as can be seen from the photos).
