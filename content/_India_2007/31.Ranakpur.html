Ranakpur

.

On the road between Udaipur and Jodhpur, Ranakpur is one of India's biggest and most important Jain temples. The main temple is called the Chaumukha Mandir (Four-Faced Temple) dedicated to Adinath and built in 1439.

The building is constructed from milk-white marble and is an astonishingly complicated series of 29 halls supported by a forest of 1,444 pillars, no two of which are alike. The interior is completely covered in intricate carving.
The devotion of its builders is shown in the complex architecture and loving carving, and the pale interior has an unparalled sense of space and harmony.
