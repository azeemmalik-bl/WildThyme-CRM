8 - May The Giant Be With You

Written by Mark Frost

Directed by David Lynch

Original Airdate: 30 September 1990
       

" Hello again. Can you see through a wall? Can you see through human skin? X-rays see through solid, or so-called solid objects. There are things in life that exist, and yet our eyes cannot see them. Have you ever seen something startling that others cannot see? Why are some things kept from our vision? Is life a puzzle?

" I am filled with questions. Sometimes my questions are answered. In my heart, I can tell if the answer is correct. I am my own judge.

" In a dream, are all the characters really you? Different aspects of you? Do answers come in dreams?

" One more thing: I grew up in the woods. I understand many things because of the woods. Trees standing together, growing alongside one another, providing so much.

" I chew pitch gum. On the outside, let's say of the ponderosa pine, sometimes pitch oozes out. Runny pitch is no good to chew. Hard, brittle pitch is no good. But in between there exists a firm, slightly crusted pitch with such a flavor. This is the pitch I chew."

 

• A shot and bleeding Agent Cooper has visions of a Giant who tells him three clues to help with the investigation:-

- There is a man in a smiling bag.

- The owls are not what they seem.

- Without chemicals, he points.

• At One-Eyed Jack’s, Audrey escapes being recognised by her father but finds that she's a prisoner there.

• Both Catherine and Josie are missing after the sawmill fire, while Shelly and Pete are in the hospital after escaping the blaze.

• After killing Jacques, Leland's hair has turned white. His mood has changed drastically: he sings and dances merrily.

• Albert Rosenfield returns to Twin Peaks, much to the dismay of Truman's team.

• Maddy has visions of BOB in her carpet.

• James remains in jail after being framed by Bobby who planted Leo's cocaine stash in his motorcycle.

• Big Ed maintains a bedside vigil for the comatose Nadine after a suicide attempt.

• Leo remains in a coma after being shot by the murderous Hank.

• Donna, following a tip from the Log Lady, decides to investigate the Double-R's Meals on Wheels program where Laura worked.

• When Cooper retires for the night, the Giant returns in a vision to tell him:-

- Don't search for all the answers at once. A path is formed by laying one stone at a time.

- One person saw the third man; three have seen him, yes, but not his body.

- One only known to you, ready now to talk.

• A stirring Ronette relives Laura's murder: she remembers BOB being at the cabin and also remembers BOB killing Laura.

Unanswered Questions

• Who is the Giant? Why is he helping Cooper, and why can he only give clues, not give Cooper the answers? Who has forbidden him to say more?

• What has happened to Catherine? Is she dead? Is she in hiding?

• It is clear that BOB killed Laura. Who is BOB? Why has no one seen him in Twin Peaks, when he was definitely present at the train car? According to Cooper's interpretation of his dream, MIKE killed BOB. Therefore, is BOB a ghost? Or is BOB inhabiting someone else's body, in much the same way that the Log Lady's husband may inhabit her log? (See Demons).

• Leland Palmer's attitude has changed. From the peak of despair, he has miraculously changed into someone with every appearance of not having a care in the world. Is this because he thinks that Laura's murderer has been "brought to justice"? Or is something more sinister afoot? (See Demons).
