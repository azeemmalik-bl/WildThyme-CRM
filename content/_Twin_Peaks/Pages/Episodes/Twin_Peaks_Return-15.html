15 - There's Some Fear in Letting Go

Written by Mark Frost and David Lynch

Directed by David Lynch

Original Airdate: 20 August 2017

Next →
  • Inspired by Dr. Amp's (Dr. Jacoby's) show, Nadine tells Ed he is free to pursue Norma.
  • Norma lets Walter buy out the RR franchise - just keeping the original diner - then accepts Ed's marriage proposal.
  • Cooper's doppelgänger arrives at the convenience store to ask Phillip Jeffries (whose voice comes from a machine) about the assassination attempt and his reference to "Judy". Jeffries tells him he has already met Judy and gives him coordinates.
  • Outside, Richard Horne attacks the doppelgänger, whom he recognises as the FBI agent his mother Audrey knew, but is subdued.
  • Gersten Hayward attempts to console an armed, suicidal Steven Burnett in the woods. Hiding from a passer-by (Cyril Pons), she hears a gunshot.
  • Agent Wilson brings Randall Headley to question a Douglas and Jane Jones and their family. However, these are not the correct Joneses, as these ones have multiple children.
  • In Las Vegas, Duncan Todd tells Roger to find Anthony Sinclair just before they are both assassinated by Chantal.
  • At the Roadhouse, James Hurley is attacked by Renee's jealous husband, Chuck, whom Freddie Sykes knocks out with his glove.
  • Freddie and James are arrested and locked up at the Twin Peaks Sheriff's Department. James notices Naido in an adjoining cell.
  • Cooper reacts to hearing Gordon Cole's name on a re-run of 'Sunset Boulevard' on TV and sticks a fork into a socket causing an electrical blackout.
  • The Log Lady calls Hawk and tells him that she is dying. He expresses his condolences, but she speaks to him about death and tells him to remember everything that she has told him and to watch for "the one". Hawk quietly says goodbye as she hangs up.
  • Audrey and Charlie continue arguing and she still doesn't leave for the Roadhouse.
  • A young woman, Ruby, crawls across the Roadhouse floor and screams.
