Geirangerfjord

Leaving Geiranger we passed awe-inspiring waterfalls including 'The Seven Sisters', 'The Suitor' and 'The Bridal Veil'. 'The Seven Sisters' and 'The Suitor' face one another across the fjord and 'The Suitor' is said to be trying to woo the sisters on the opposite side. Legend has it that 'The Bridal Veil' is so named because it falls delicately over one rocky edge and, when backlit by the sun, it has the appearance of a thin veil over the rocks..
