The clients wanted a garden which was lush, in which they could entertain family and friends, which had several seating areas and which provided height and year-round colour. They were delighted with the final result.

 

 

before - house and garden

after - house and garden

The garden was sunny and the soil extremely free-draining so planting was limited to varities which tolerate these conditions.

The three birch trees provide a focal point in the centre of the garden - with beautiful white bark throughout the year.
