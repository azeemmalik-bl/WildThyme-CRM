17 - Dispute Between Brothers

Written by Tricia Brock

Directed by Tina Rathborne

Original Airdate: 8 December 1990
       

" Complications set in - yes, complications. How many times have we heard: 'it's simple'? Nothing is simple. We live in a world where nothing is simple. Each day, just when we think we have a handle on things, suddenly some new element is introduced and everything is complicated once again. What is the secret? What is the secret to simplicity, to the pure and simple life? Are our appetites, our desires undermining us? Is the cart in front of the horse? "

 

• Three days after Leland's death, following his confession to having killed Laura Palmer while under the control of the evil spirit BOB, Sarah tries to adjust herself to this turn of events with Cooper's explanation.

• A wake is held for Leland Palmer where Dougie Milford, publisher of the Twin Peaks Gazette, antagonises his brother, Mayor Dwayne Milford.

• Big Ed and Dr. Jacoby persuade the Vice-Principal of Twin Peaks High School to enroll Nadine as a student to satisfy her delusion that she's still in high school.

• Audrey says goodbye to Cooper who tells her how his first love died while he should have been protecting her, and how his then partner Windom Earle went mad.

• Bobby unsuccessfully tries to blackmail the distraught Ben Horne with the incriminating tape of him talking with Leo, while Audrey gives Bobby some advice about how to confront her father.

• Catherine pays a visit to the sheriff's station and tells Truman that a "guardian angel" saved her life at the sawmill fire.

• Dick Tremayne has given up smoking and embraces fatherhood.

• As Cooper prepares to leave Twin Peaks with the Laura Palmer case closed, a new twist comes when Roger Hardy, an agent with FBI Internal Affairs, and a Canadian Mountie named Preston King, show up and suspend Cooper for his unauthorised actions in Canada.

• Mountie King also reveals that Cooper's rescue of Audrey Horne from One-Eyed Jack’s ruined a sting operation set up to arrest Jean Renault. The drugs used for the sting will be subsequently found in Cooper's car.

• Norma's mother, Vivian, leaves town after revealing that she's the M.T. Wentz that everyone talks about and the one responsible for the bad review of the Double-R Diner.

• Hank takes Ernie Niles off on an impromptu "hunting trip" to One-Eyed Jack’s where they meet with Jean Renault who wants them to traffic some cocaine for him.

• Josie Packard reappears and collapses at Truman's house.

• On Cooper's fishing trip, Major Briggs reveals the existence of the White Lodge before he is mysteriously abducted.

Unanswered Questions

• Where has Josie Packard been? Why has she come back?

• Who or what has abducted Major Briggs?
