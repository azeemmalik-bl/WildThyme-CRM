7 - There's A Body All Right

Written by Mark Frost and David Lynch

Directed by David Lynch

Original Airdate: 18 June 2017

  • Jerry Horne is stoned in the woods and phones brother Ben to say his car has been stolen.
  • Hawk and Frank examine the diary pages, which describe a dream in which Annie Blackburn tells Laura that the "good" Dale is trapped in the Black Lodge.
  • Frank calls Doc Hayward who recalls that, after returning from the Black Lodge, Cooper sneaked out of intensive care, where Audrey Horne was in a coma.
  • Lieutenant Cindy Knox arrives in Buckhorn and learns that Major Briggs' fingerprints come from a body in its forties, not seventies.
  • Albert and Gordon convince Diane to speak to Cooper in prison. She is upset by the conversation and tells Gordon the man is not Cooper.
  • Cooper's doppelgänger ("Mr. C") blackmails Warden Murphy (by another reference to "Mr. Strawberry" and a dismembered dog) into releasing both him and Ray.
  • Police visit Cooper at Dougie's workplace to question him about his destroyed car. As he leaves with Janey-E, Ike runs at them with a gun, but Cooper expertly disarms him while having a vision of the Arm.
  • In Twin Peaks, the owner of the truck driven by Richard in the hit-and-run fails to arrive for a meeting with Andy.
  • Beverly Paige gives Ben Horne the key which Jade mailed back from Las Vegas. He recognises it as from the room where an attempt was made on Cooper's life.
  • They both try to locate the source of a hum in Ben's office, then Beverly returns home to her terminally ill husband, Tom.
  • At the Roadhouse, Jean-Michel Renault receives a call about two prostitutes he sent to a customer, only to learn that they were only 15-years old. He denies any responsibility.
  • A young man (Bing) bursts into the Double R Diner, looking for Billy.
