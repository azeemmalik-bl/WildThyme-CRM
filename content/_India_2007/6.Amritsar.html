Amritsar - Hotel

.

We stayed in the delightful Svaasa Hotel - a smart refurbishment of a 200 year old British Colonial Haveli, now plush with its characteristic boutique rooms and suites, fully equipped with modern amenities.

The open courtyard has landscaped greenery and the rooftop has vegetation too. The Mehras, the Royal family descendants who personally manage and run this place, make it a rich experience.
