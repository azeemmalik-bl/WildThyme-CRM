Cambodia - Ta Prohm

Now seemingly better known for being the location where Angelina Jolie got her kit off and did the Tomb Raider thing, Ta Prohm is truly magnificent, its crumbling towers and walls locked in the slow muscular embrace of vast root systems. Dating from 1186, or to put it another way, when Henry II was on the English throne ie: before Richard the Lionheart. My favourite temple of them all.
