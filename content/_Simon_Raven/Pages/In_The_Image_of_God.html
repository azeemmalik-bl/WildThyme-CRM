In the Image of God
Dramatis personæ
Theodosia ('Thea') Canteloupe
Marchioness Cantaloupe, who gives birth to a female heir, sired by Marius Stern.
Aunt Flo
Aunt to the dead schoolboy Palairet and now confidante to Theodosia Canteloupe.
Jeremy Morrison
'Squire of Luffham by Whereham. Younger son of Peter Morrison.
Marius Stern
Son of Isobel Stern, nephew to, the now-dead, Provost Llewyllyn.
Carmilla Salinger
Fellow of Lancaster College; twin sister to Theodosia (now Lady Canteloupe).
Lady Nausikaa Sarum
Child of Marchioness Cantaloupe and Marius Stern.
Teresa ('Tessa') Malcolm
'Niece' of Maisie Malcolm, proprietress of Buttock's Hotel.
La Soeur
Doctor. Previously appeared in The Rich Pay Late, Come Like Shadows, The Face of The Waters and New Seed For Old.
Raisley Conyngham
A schoolmaster. Dubious.
'Mrs.' Maisie Malcolm
Proprietress (with Fielding Gray) of Buttock's Hotel.
Major Fielding Gray
A novelist, co-owner of Buttock's Hotel and confidant of Marius Stern.
Terpsichore
Transvestite male cook in the Stern household. Previously known as "Crystal" and "Ethel". Takes over the management of Buttock's Hotel while Maisie Malcolm is unwell. Previously known as Geddes, Squadron Barber in The Sabre Squadron.
Luigino

Sicilian cook to Piero Caspar.

Piero Caspar

Sicilian, adopted heir to Ptolomaeos Tunne and now owner of Tunne Hall. Appointed to a Fellowship of Lancaster College by Provost Llewyllyn.

Mr. Trapp

Head Porter at Marcian College who remembers Raisley Conyngham.

Dr. Davie Gamp, D.D.

Registrar at Marcian College, uncle of Jonathan Gamp (see Friends In Low Places).

Len
Private Secretary to the Provost of Lancaster College.
Sir Jacquiz Helmutt
An art baron. Now Provost of Lancaster College.
Miss Jesty Hyphen
Now-retired Classical mistress at Brydales School who previously taught Raisley Conyngham and who went on holiday with him to the Languedoc.

Major Giles Glastonbury

Friend of Lord Canteloupe. Previously appeared in Sound The Retreat, The Sabre Squadron and Before the Cock Crow.

Abel Thynne

Former Head Lad at Ullacote.

Lord Luffham

Peter Morrison, father of Jeremy and Governor of Oudenarde House.
The Chamberlain
Peter Morrison's manservant at Luffham; formerly manservant to Canteloupe; mistakenly received a Christmas card from Giles Glastonbury intended for Raisley Conyngham.
Gat-toothed Jenny

A stable lass who had previously introduced Marius Stern to the 'pleasures of the flesh' while working for Raisley Conyngham. Later works for Lord Canteloupe before dying while playing 'Fives' on his court.

Lord Canteloupe
Captain the Most Honorable Marquess Cantelope of the Aestuary of the Severn. Husband of Thea Canteloupe.
Danny Chead
A jockey. Son of 'Corpy' Chead who dies by being impaled on railings at Regis Priory racecourse.
Dorchester
Steward at Regis Priory racecourse.
Isobel Stern
Mother of Marius and Rosie Stern, currently living in France with Jo-Jo Guiscard.
Josephine ('Jo-Jo') Guiscard
Wife of antiquarian Jean-Marie Guiscard, enjoying a 'platonic' relationship with Isobel Stern.
Jean-Marie Guiscard
Antiquarian studying the Cathars in the Languedoc region.
Oenone
Their precocious three-year old daughter.
Milo Hedley
A schoolboy under Conyngham's charge.
Dean ffoliott-Hume of Trinity
Spurned disciple ("gross yet dandified") of Raisley Conyngham.
Jacques-Emile Gagneac
Curator of the Museum of Antique Sculpture in Narbonne.
   
Plot Summary

Raisley Conyngham, Classics teacher at Marius Stern's school, exerts a powerful influence over his young student who, despite this, is no defenceless victim.

Marius has a ruthless streak and an ability to sidestep tests and traps that are laid for him. Which is just as well because everybody is after something from him ...
