Fox Glacier & Mount Cook
     
 

Fox Glacier was named in 1872 after a visit by then Prime Minister of New Zealand, Sir William Fox. Following the passage of the Ngai Tahu Claims Settlement Act 1998, the name of the glacier was officially altered to Fox Glacier / Te Moeka o Tuawe. We witnessed a dramatic collapse of the foot of the glacier (see video) accompanied by the wild ululations of South Korean tourists.

Mount Cook (Aoraki) is the highest mountain in New Zealand at 12,218 feet. Often shrouded in clouds we were thrilled to have it revealed to us while having supper at our camp site.
