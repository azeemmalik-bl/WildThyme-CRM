Cambodia - Baphuon
     
 

Described as the 'world's largest jigsaw puzzle', Baphuon was being painstakingly being taken apart piece-by-piece and restored by archeologists before the civil war, but their records were destroyed during the Khmer Rouge regime leaving experts with 300,000 stones to put back in place. After years of research, this temple has been partially restored. On the western side, the retaining wall of the second level was fashioned, in the 16th century, into a reclining Buddha 60m in length.
