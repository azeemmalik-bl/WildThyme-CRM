11 - There's Fire Where You Are Going

Written by Mark Frost and David Lynch

Directed by David Lynch

Original Airdate: 23 July 2017

Next →
  • A group of children discover a bloodied Miriam Sullivan crawling from the underbrush. She obviously did not die at the hands of Richard Horne.
  • Becky learns Steven has been cheating on her with Gersten Hayward. She drives to Gersten's apartment and shoots through the door, but the couple are elsewhere.
  • At the Double R Diner, Becky's parents, Shelly and Bobby, discuss the incident with her. Red arrives and Shelly leaves to kiss him. A child fires a gun through the diner window, causing a commotion.
  • At the location where Hastings met Major Briggs (2240 Sycamore), the FBI finds Ruth Davenport's headless body with coordinates written on one arm. Gordon sees woodsmen in a vortex but is drawn back by Albert. One of the woodsmen sneaks up on Hastings in the car and kills him. Diane also saw the woodsman but did nothing.
  • While Hawk and Frank study an ancient map, the Log Lady calls Hawk and tells him "There's fire where you are going".
  • Dougie's boss sends Cooper to give the Mitchums a $30 million cheque after realising their claim is legitimate. The Mitchums plan to kill Cooper but, after Bradley has a prophetic dream, they decide he is not their enemy and take him for drinks, where Cooper is recognised by the Slot Machine Lady who is now richer and has reunited with her son. Cooper also reacts to the words "damn good" while eating cherry pie.
