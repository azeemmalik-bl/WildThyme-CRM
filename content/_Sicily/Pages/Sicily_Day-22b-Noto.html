Day 22 - 16 May 2018 (Noto)
     
 

Astonishing buildings either side of this street: on one side it is the Villadorata Palace (built 1733) and the other is the Casa Vinicola (house of wine makers from San Giovanni) which is still in its original form - upstairs is now a museum (featuring artefacts owned by the family - including their own private poison bottles) and downstairs a restaurant.

It also looks as if Siegfried & Roy (well at least Siegfried since Roy was attacked by his tiger in 2003) have come to Noto. Plus some very non-PC ceramics ...
