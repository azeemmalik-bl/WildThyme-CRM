Day 33 - 27 May 2018 (Colombier-le-Vieux)

Joy of Joys! We discover that Colombier-le-Vieux was used as a location for the filming of the 1972 TV series Clochemerle (satirising the conflicts between Catholics and Republicans in the French Third Republic after the installation of a urinal in a small town square). Adapted by Galton & Simpson of Steptoe and Son fame, I remember being allowed to stay up and watch it (despite its subject matter) as it was French and my parents therefore thought it “literary” ...
