21 - Double Play

Written by Scott Frost

Directed by Uli Edel

Original Airdate: 2 February 1991

“ The heart – it is a physical organ, we all know. But how much more an emotional organ – this we also know. Love, like blood, flows from the heart. Are blood and love related? Does a heart pump blood as it pumps love? Is love the blood of the universe? ”

  • Cooper discusses with Truman the opening moves by his former FBI partner, Windom Earle, over the discovery of a dead body which is the opening move of a deadly chess game where, when each pawn is taken, a person dies.
  • Audrey Horne strikes a business deal with Bobby Briggs and asks Jerry Horne for help concerning Ben's delusion in re-enacting the Civil War, while Dr. Jacoby persuades Ben to push for a Confederate victory.
  • Leo Johnson comes back to vicious life and attempts to kill Shelly; she stabs him in the leg and he flees into the woods.
  • Andy confides to Lucy about his and Dick's suspicion concerning Little Nicky. Lucy then asks Dr. Hayward for help who then finally clears the mystery.
  • At the Marsh estate, James Hurley meets Evelyn's husband for the first time, who seems to be a nice guy, and becomes more confused about his feelings for Evelyn.
  • Back in Twin Peaks, Big Ed confides in 'Doc' Hayward about Nadine dating Mike Nelson.
  • Cooper also confides to Truman the frightening, tragic history of his involvement with Earle's wife Caroline that may have pushed Earle over the edge.
  • In the nearby town, Donna arrives with money for James, and instead meets Evelyn who tells her that James has left town already.
  • Major Briggs staggers into the Sheriff's office and confides in Cooper and Truman about his work for the Air Force's classified search for the so-called White Lodge. He believes he was taken to the White Lodge during his abduction.
  • Dr. Jacoby also clears Lana Budding of any misdeeds concerning the death of Dougie Milford, but it takes some persuasion to convince the elderly and senile Mayor Milford.
  • Catherine Martell unveils an astonishing secret to a dumbfounded Pete – Andrew Packard is alive – and they let him in on their plans to bring down Josie by contacting her former boss / lover, the ruthless South African businessman Thomas Eckhardt, who arrives in Twin Peaks looking for Josie.
  • Evelyn Marsh's husband is killed in a car crash. Malcolm is not her brother. James has been set up. He makes his escape and meets Donna who is still in town looking for him.
  • After Leo escapes into the woods, pursued by owls, he arrives at a cabin housing the deranged Windom Earle

Unanswered Questions

  • How did Leo Johnson come back out of his coma?
  • What will be Windom Earle's next move?
