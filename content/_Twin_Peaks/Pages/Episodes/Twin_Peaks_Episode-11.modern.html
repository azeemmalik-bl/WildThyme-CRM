11 - Laura's Secret Diary

Written by Jerry Stahl, Mark Frost, Harley Peyton & Robert Engels

Directed by Todd Holland

Original Airdate: 20 October 1990

Next →

"Miscommunication sometimes leads to arguments, and arguments sometimes lead to fights. Anger is usually present in arguments and fights. Anger is an emotion, usually classified as a negative emotion. Negative emotions can cause severe problems in our environment and to the health of our body.

"Happiness, usually classified as a positive emotion, can bring good health to our body, and spread positive vibrations into our environment. Sometimes when we are ill, we are not on our best behavior. By ill, I mean any of the following: physically ill, emotionally ill, mentally ill, and/or spiritually ill."

  • Cooper and Sheriff Truman arrest Leland after he confesses that he was the one who murdered Jacques Renault in his hospital bed as well as the one who attacked Dr. Jacoby that night under the delusion that he was protecting Laura.
  • Andy asks Dr. Hayward about taking a sperm test to see if he really is sterile.
  • Hank Jennings encourages Norma to spruce up the Double-R Diner when news circulates that a shadowy travel writer and food critic named M.T. Wentz, whom no one has ever met, may be coming to Twin Peaks.
  • Meanwhile, Jean Renault delivers a videotape of the captive Audrey to Ben Horne and demands a huge ransom for her return and he wants Cooper to be the drop-off man.
  • Ben Horne is prepared to help Renault kill Cooper if it will get his daughter back.
  • On returning to One-Eyed Jack's, Renault murders Emory Battis for treating the captive Audrey badly. Cooper asks Truman to help him with investigating Audrey's kidnapping.
  • Donna continues to meet with Harold Smith who shows her Laura Palmer's secret diary which Laura gave to him for safekeeping, which increases Donna's interest.
  • Josie returns to Twin Peaks claiming to have been in Seattle on business where she learns from Pete that Catherine died in the sawmill fire. Josie also refuses to give Truman an explanation about where she was during this time.
  • A mysterious Japanese businessman, Tojamura, checks into the Great Northern Hotel.
  • The mysterious Asian man, identifying himself as Jonathan Lee, Josie's "cousin" from Hong Kong, meets with Josie and informs her that her job in Twin Peaks of collecting the insurance from the sawmill fire is nearly done and that their boss wants them back in Hong Kong soon.
  • Josie is working to some sort of deadline, one which Jonathan is there to enforce. It appears that she is working for a "Mr. Eckhardt".
  • Jonathan also beats up Hank Jennings for threatening Josie.

Unanswered Questions

  • Who is Jonathan? Why is he in Twin Peaks?
  • What is Josie's "mission", and why is she working for "Mr. Eckhardt"? Who is he?
  • Who is Tojamura? Is he the mystery M.T. Wentz?
  • Will Cooper suspect Ben Horne's request is a trap?
