23 - The Condemned Woman

Written by Tricia Brock

Directed by Lesli Linka Glatter

Original Airdate: 16 February 1991

“ A hotel. A nightstand. A drawer pull on the drawer. A drawer pull of a nightstand in the room of a hotel. What could possibly be happening on or in this drawer pull? How many drawer pulls exist in this world? Thousands, maybe millions.

“ What is a drawer pull? This drawer pull – why is it featured so prominently in a life or in a death of one woman who was caught in a web of power? Can a victim of power end in any way connected to a drawer pull? How can this be? ”

  • Cooper and Truman try to outline the moves left by Windom Earle by investigating more of his past.
  • Josie collapses when she sees Andrew Packard, whom she thought was dead, having breakfast with Catherine.
  • Truman arrests Hank for parole violation and attempting to kill Leo, but Hank reveals that he and Josie were the ones who conspired to murder Andrew Packard.
  • Audrey meets a potential new beau in the form of John Justice Wheeler, a business associate of the newly-sane Ben Horne, who comes to town to help thwart Catherine's development plans for Ghostwood Estates by holding a campaign to save the endangered Pine Weasel which resides on the development grounds.
  • Windom Earle tears a poem (Shelley's Love's Philosophy) into three and sends a piece each to Audrey, Donna and Shelly as a tantalising puzzle to the three women.
  • Nadine admits to Ed that she is in love with Mike.
  • Cooper pleads with Josie Packard for the truth as Catherine looks on. She says she escaped from Jonathan at the airport but didn't kill him.
  • Shelly comes back to work at the Double-R Diner where she finds the message from Windom Earle.
  • Ed Hurley proposes marriage to Norma Jennings who then visits Hank before he's sent back to prison to tell him that she wants a divorce. She refuses to give him an alibi regarding his shooting of Leo.
  • Agent Rosenfeld leaves town but persuades Cooper to arrest Josie before she plans to escape.
  • Donna finds herself saying goodbye to James who, although cleared of murder charges, tells her that he must follow his own path.
  • Andrew Packard reveals that he's alive to both Josie and Thomas Eckhardt to try to bring them to kill each other as part of Catherine's evil plans.
  • Catherine calls Cooper to warn him that Josie is heading for the Great Northern to kill Eckhardt. Cooper bursts into Eckhardt's room after hearing a gun shot. Eckhardt dies and, as Truman arrives, Josie dies of an apparent heart attack. Cooper has a vision of Killer BOB and The Man From Another Place while Josie's soul appears to be imprisoned in a drawer pull on the nightstand.

Unanswered Questions

  • What is WIndom Earle's plan for the three women?
  • Why and how has Josie's soul been imprisoned?
