22 - Slaves And Masters

Written by Harley Peyton and Robert Engels

Directed by Diane Keaton

Original Airdate: 9 February 1991

“ A death mask. Is there a reason for a death mask? It is barely a physical resemblance – in death, the muscles so relaxed, the face so without the animating spark. A death mask is almost an intrusion on a beautiful memory. And yet ... who could throw away the casting of a loved one? Who would not want to study it longingly, as the distant freight train blows its mournful tone? ”

  • James Hurley becomes a fugitive when Evelyn's husband is found murdered.
  • Donna Hayward makes a desperate plea to save James' life while Evelyn is pressured by her abusive boyfriend, Malcolm, to continue their charade of playing the "victims".
  • Bobby and Shelly give their statement about Leo's rampage and disappearance, and also divulge that Hank Jennings that was the one who shot Leo on the night of the sawmill fire.
  • Albert Rosenfield arrives in Twin Peaks to deliver to Cooper and Truman the classified file on the fugitive Windom Earle's motivations – "Springfield, Kansas City, Lawton, Dallas, Jackson" and "white veil, garter, white slippers, pearl necklace, white dress".
  • At his cabin, the flute-playing Windom Earle holds the half-witted Leo against his will with an electric collar while he is forcibly recruited as Earle's henchman to help him stalk Cooper.
  • Big Ed and Norma finally consummate their relationship, while Nadine finds out and reacts with indifference.
  • Truman questions Josie about a murder (that of "Jonathan") back in Seattle.
  • Cooper finally discovers that it was Josie who shot him at the Great Northern Hotel after finding a black vicuña coat in her dry-cleaning and finds unexpected help in Pete Martell in matching wits with Windom Earle in his deadly game of chess.
  • Shelly asks for (and gets) her job back at the Double-R Diner.
  • Catherine Martell dangles a helpless Josie Packard as booty before the eyes of an extremely dangerous man – the evil-hearted Thomas Eckhardt.
  • Benjamin Horne changes the course of history at Appomattox while Audrey, Bobby, Jerry and Dr. Jacoby dress up in Civil War costumes to implement the 'Appomattox Scenario' to finally bring Ben back to reality.
  • Evelyn kills Malcolm in a stand-off with Donna.
  • Windom Earle leaves a nasty reminder for Cooper at the Great Northern Hotel.

Unanswered Questions

  • But WHY did Josie shoot Cooper?
  • How will the Josie / Eckhardt situation resolve itself?
  • What is Windom Earle's intention with Cooper?
