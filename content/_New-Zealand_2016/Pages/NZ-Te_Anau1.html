Road to Te Anau

After yesterday's travels through the Southern Alps featuring mountains called Mount Awful, Mount Dreadful and the surprising Mount Doris, today we left Wanaka and took the steep and windy road over 'The Remarkables' peaks down to Queenstown where we stopped for breaksfast and provisions. A scenic stop for lunch at Lake Wakatipu before we finally wove our way into Te Anau for a beer and supper.
