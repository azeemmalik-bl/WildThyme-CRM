Plot Summary

The book opens with the christening of Marquess Canteloupe's son and heir, Sarum of Old Sarum. The ceremony, attended by the godparents and the real father, Fielding Gray, is not without drama.

The christening introduces a bizarre cast of eccentric chracters and complicated relationships. We meet the brilliant but troublesome teenager Marius Stern, who is suspended from his boarding school for hitting another boy. Scientist Ptolemaeos Tunne, with the assistance of Jeremy Morrison, attempts to get to the bottom of Marius' behaviour. Marius has a crush on Jeremy and persuades him to share his bed, though nothing sexual happens. Meanwhile, when Marius' parents are kidnapped, apparently without reason, events take a turn for the worse.
