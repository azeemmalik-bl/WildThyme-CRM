Vietnam - Mekong Delta

Away from the horrors at the market, we enjoyed rides on punts on the Mekong Delta and then scarily (as they swerved so erratically) on the back of motorcycle delivery vans. Vietnam pointy hats obligatory for all.

Then to an extraordinary hand-made brick factory complete with kilns heated by burning rice husks and a clay blender cum shaper which looks totally Heath Robinson. Never has automation seemed such a positive option ...
