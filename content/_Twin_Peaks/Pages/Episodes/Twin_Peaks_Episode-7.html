7 - The Last Evening

Written by Mark Frost

Directed by Mark Frost

Original Airdate: 24 May 1990

Next →

“A drunken man walks in a way that is quite impossible for a sober man to imitate, and vice versa. An evil man has a way, no matter how clever-to the trained eye, his way will show itself.

Am I being too secretive? No. One can never answer questions at the wrong moment. Life, like music, has a rhythm. This particular song will end with three sharp sounds, like deathly drumbeats.”

  • Dr. Jacoby heads for his rendezvous with "Laura Palmer" and suffers a heart attack when he is attacked by an unknown assailant.
  • Nadine takes an overdose of pills.
  • At One-Eyed Jack's, Cooper's 'sting' works on Jacques Renault. He discovers that Laura and Ronette met Jacques and Leo at Jacques' cabin. Laura had sex with both Leo and Jacques. Waldo attacked her in a fit of jealously. The plastic fragment was from a One-Eyed Jack's gambling chip that Leo told her to bite. Leo hit Jacques who passed out and, when he awoke, Leo and the girls had gone - presumably to the abandoned train car.
  • Deputy Andy shoots Jacques Renault as he tries to escape capture. Lucy is impressed and tells Andy she is pregnant.
  • Hank Jennings' evil influence spreads, engulfing Josie Packard. It appears Hank murdered Andrew Packard on Josie's orders. He went to prison to cover his tracks, and was paid $90,000 by Josie. However, he still wants more, and is prepared to blackmail her to get it.
  • Leland Palmer, his mental state deteriorating rapidly, kills Jacques Renault in the hospital.
  • Leo takes his revenge and leaves Shelly to die in the sawmill, which he set alight. Both Shelly and Catherine were in the sawmill, attempting to escape. Pete Martell enters the burning building looking for Catherine.
  • Hank shoots Leo Johnson (on Ben Horne's orders) after Leo set the sawmill alight.
  • But Bobby Briggs sees Hank shoot Leo.
  • Cooper believes that Leo was Laura's killer.
  • Back at the hotel, Cooper is shot by an unknown assailant.
