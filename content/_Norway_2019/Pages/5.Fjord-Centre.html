Geiranger Fjord Centre

The Geiranger Fjord Centre provides the history of the mountains, the fjords and local people, including an interactive multimedia show, "From Mountain to Fjord".

 
Entrance to the exhibition was via a mock-up of an old paddle steamer which used to sail on the fjord.
 
 
Then a water-soaked climb down past the gushing waterfall of the Geirangelva river which tumbles into the fjord at the bottom.
