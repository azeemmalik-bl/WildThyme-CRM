Day 14 - 8 May 2018 (Florence)
     
 

An early morning start in order to catch David before the hoards arrive. And some lovely tacky merchandise which tempted me as I departed.

And then onto Piazza Santa Maria Novella which is an asymmetric medieval square where the ‘Palio dei Cocchi’ (Chariot Race), instituted by Cosimo I, took place - the obelisks in the square show the route of the race. The church of S. Maria Novella (built between 1246-1360) is almost more of a gallery than a church with stunning works by Giotto, Masaccio, Paolo Uccello and Andrea di Firenze.

Asparagus risotto!
