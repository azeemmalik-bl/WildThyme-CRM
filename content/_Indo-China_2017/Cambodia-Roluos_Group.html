Cambodia - Bakong Temple

Our first temple visit at Angkor, the Bakong Temple in the Roluos Group is the oldest dating from the 8th century. Built and dedicated to Shiva by Indravarman I, it is a representation of Mt. Meru and served as the city's central temple. Security at the whole Angkor site is a priority and we were each given a 3-day non-transferable pass to the Temple Complex.
