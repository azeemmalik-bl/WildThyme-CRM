9 – This Is The Chair

Written by Mark Frost and David Lynch
Directed by David Lynch
Original Airdate: 9 July 2017

  • Cooper's doppelgänger ("Mr. C") meets hitmen Hutch and Chantal and orders them to kill Warden Murphy.
  • He sends a text to Diane ("Around the dinner table, the conversation is lively") and calls Duncan Todd to ask if he has done "it" yet.
  • Dougie's boss tells the Fusco Brothers that Dougie sometimes has episodes due to a car accident. They wonder if Dougie is under witness protection as the Las Vegas police can find no record of Dougie Jones before 1997. They take fingerprints and DNA from his coffee mug.
  • Ike leaves a phone message for "JT" and is arrested as he leaves his motel room.
  • Bobby visits his mother with Frank Truman and Hawk to ask about Cooper; her husband Major Briggs long ago foretold their arrival, and she gives them a cylinder hidden in a chair containing a location (Jack Rabbit's Palace), a time (2:53), a date and the transmission Briggs intercepted with Cooper's name printed twice. Bobby knows the location from his childhood.
  • The FBI stops in Buckhorn to examine the corpse with Major Briggs' fingerprints.
  • Hastings says he never killed Ruth and tells Tammy Preston that he and Ruth visited Briggs in another dimension, where he had been "hibernating" for years, and witnessed his beheading as he was saying "Cooper, Cooper".
  • Johnny Horne injures himself, and Jerry Horne hallucinates that his foot is talking.
  • Ella and Chloe talk at the Roadhouse about 'Zebra' and 'Penguin' and discuss how Ella has been fired from her job for being high.
