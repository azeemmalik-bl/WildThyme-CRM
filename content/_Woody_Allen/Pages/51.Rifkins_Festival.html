Plot
     
The film tells the story of an American couple attending the San Sebastian International Film Festival. The charm of the small city in the north of Spain and the charm of the world of cinema soon seduce the couple. She has a fling with a brilliant French film director, while he falls in love with a young Spanish resident in the city.
     
 
Filming
  
     
 

In February 2019, Amazon Studios dropped Allen from a five-picture deal to produce and finance films, following the revival of the sexual abuse allegations made against him by his adopted daughter Dylan in 1992. That month, it was announced Allen would write and direct the film, with Jaume Roures producing under his Mediapro banner. In June 2019, Gina Gershon, Christoph Waltz, Elena Anaya, Louis Garrel, Sergi López and Wallace Shawn joined the cast. In July 2020, Richard Kind announced he would appear in the film.

Principal photography began on 10 July 2019, in San Sebastián, Spain and ended on 16 August, a week ahead of schedule.
