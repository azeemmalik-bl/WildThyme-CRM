13 - What Story Is That, Charlie?

Written by Mark Frost and David Lynch

Directed by David Lynch

Original Airdate: 6 August 2017

Next →
  • The Mitchum Brothers thank Dougie and Bushnell with gifts - including matching BMW cars and a play gym for Sonny Jim.
  • Anthony Sinclair tells Duncan Todd that the killing of Dougie Jones failed. Todd gives him one day to sort this out. Sinclair meets a corrupt policeman to obtain poison to kill Dougie but, at the last minute, flushes the poisoned coffee away and confesses his collusion with Todd to Bushnell.
  • The Las Vegas police dismiss Dougie's fingerprint analysis as it shows he had only been released from a South Dakota prison two days before and was an FBI Agent.
  • Cooper's doppelgänger ("Mr. C") arrives at "the Farm" in Montana, where Ray is with a gang. Mr. C defeats Renzo, the gang leader, in an arm-wrestling match then kills him while the gang (including Richard Horne) watch. Ray takes out the Owl Cave Ring saying that he was supposed to put it on Mr. C after the killing. Ray is quizzed about the assassination attempt and tells that it was Phillip Jeffries who ordered the killing as Mr. C has "something inside" that he wanted. Ray hands over the coordinates and reveals Jeffries' last-known location. He is then killed. The ring disappears from his finger and appears in the Red Room, followed by himself. MIKE places the ring on its pedestal.
  • Hutch and Chantal drive through Utah and discuss Mormons.
  • At the RR, Bobby Briggs meets Big Ed and Norma while Bobby tells them that he has come across some belongings of his father. Norma meets Walter Lawford, who helped franchise her business and is trying, discreetly, to change it.
  • At 'Run Silent Run Drapes', Nadine is visited by Dr. Jacoby after he spots one of his shovels in the window. He expresses his admiration for the decoration and she praises him and his show. They reflect on their last meeting at a supermarket during a storm.
  • Sarah Palmer watches a loop of a boxing match.
  • Audrey tells Charlie she does not know who or where she is and cannot leave the house.
  • James, performing at the Roadhouse, moves Renee to tears.
