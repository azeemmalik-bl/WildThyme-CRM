Day 7 - 1 May 2018 (Lucerne)
     
 

Stopped for a delicious lunch in Lucerne and was snapped next to the Kapellbrücke (originally built in 1333 but largely replaced in 1993 after much of it burned down due to a discarded cigarette - bloody smokers!!) which I had wanted to visit ever since I received a box of Swiss chocolate as a child with photos of bridges (Lindt? Suchard? - damn, can’t remember).

Plus a lot of half-timbered buildings with painted frontages and the ever-present Goethe looming over Sammy and Seán.
