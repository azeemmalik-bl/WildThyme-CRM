Aboard Ship - Egypt 2008
to

There is nothing quite so relaxing as sitting up on deck and watching other boats on the Nile weaving in and out of the slipstream of ours and also viewing life on the banks of the river. The soundtrack was Philip Glass' Akhnaten.

Several of the guests on the boat appear to be doing their own impersonation of Pharaohs and mummies ...
We were lucky to have an outside cabin with a small balcony - lovely to have the doors open first thing in the morning.
 
The cabin staff always had a surprise for us when we returned to our rooms creating creatures out of the bedding and clothing found in our room. Ingenious and very amusing.
 
 
Great fun! A dressing-up evening with games ...
 
 
Potato racing ...
 
The final game involved having to dress your partner as a Mummy using only toilet paper ...
