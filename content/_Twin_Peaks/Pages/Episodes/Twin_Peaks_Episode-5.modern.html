5 - Cooper's Dreams

Written by Mark Frost

Directed by Lesli Linka Glatter

Original Airdate: 10 May 1990

Next →

“I play my part on my stage. I tell what I can to form the perfect answer. But that answer cannot come before all are ready to hear. So I tell what I can to form the perfect answer.

Sometimes my anger at the fire is evident. Sometimes it is not anger, really. It may appear as such, but could it be a clue? The fire I speak of is not a kind fire.”

  • Audrey Horne takes a job at her father's department store to help Cooper with his investigations
  • Hank Jennings returns from prison to the Double-R Diner.
  • James Hurley and Donna Hayward enlist Maddy in their search for Laura's killer. She finds a cassette tape hidden in her bedroom.
  • The blood on Leo Johnson's shirt (found at Jacques Renault's flat) is not that of Laura Palmer.
  • Cooper and Truman have tea with the Log Lady and discover a macabre crime scene in the woods. It seems that the Log Lady's Log is capable of observing events.
  • The Log Lady had been expecting Cooper and Truman to call; she even said that they were late. Does this imply some sort of psychic ability on her part?
  • Laura and Ronette were both present at Jacques Renault's cabin in the woods.
  • Waldo, Jacques' mynah bird, witnessed the events at the cabin and Cooper finds a One-Eyed Jack's token with the "J" missing.
  • Audrey spies on her father's secret assignation wtih Catherine Martell in his office.
  • Leo Johnson is attacked by Hank Jennings at his cabin. He is then shot by Shelly when he tries to hit her.
  • Benjamin Horne meets Josie in his office. She has found the second (false) set of accounts.
  • Cooper returns to his hotel room to find Audrey waiting for him in bed.

Unanswered Questions

  • Why does the Log Lady alone hear the "voice" of her log? Is there in fact anything there, or is she just hallucinating / dreaming that she hears a voice? Perhaps the Log is a link with another world. Truman says that there is something mysterious in the local woods; perhaps the Log is a link to these powers. Another possibility is that the Log is inhabited by the spirit of Margaret's husband. Maybe he communicates with her through the Log. Perhaps he is in the White Lodge,
  • How did Laura and Ronette get from the cabin to the train car? Did Jacques take them? (See Lonely Souls).
  • What useful information will be gained from Waldo the bird?
  • What is Hank's connection to Josie? Whatever link there is between them, she seems very scared of him. Perhaps he is in possession of information that could damage her, or perhaps he has some other hold over her, perhaps related to the killing of the vagrant that caused him to be jailed. (See The Last Evening).
  • Now that James and Donna have Maddy's help, what are they going to do? (See Realization Time).
  • Does Ben Horne know what Battis is doing? If not, how will he react when he finds out?
