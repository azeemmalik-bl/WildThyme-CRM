Felucca Trip - Egypt 2008
to

A more restful afternoon when we took a trip from Aswan out onto the Nile in a felucca. It was thrilling to see the beautiful sails of this impressive boat fill with the breeze as we were swept out across the river to view Elephantine Isle (with its extraordinary elephant-like rock formations).,

 
 
The ornate facade and lush gardens surrounding the Old Cateract Hotel are one of Aswan's major river bank landmarks and impossible to miss when taking a sightseeing sailing trip around Aswan aboard a felucca. The hotel's biggest claim to fame is that Agatha Christie wrote part of Death on the Nile while staying here, and the hotel also featured in the movie based on the novel.
