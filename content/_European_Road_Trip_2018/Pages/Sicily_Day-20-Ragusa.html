Day 20 - 14 May 2018 (Ragusa)
     
 

Filling your wine bottles at the vineyard - pretty decent stuff at €2 per bottle - changing a wheel after a burst tyre and a super trip into Ragusa to take ownership of a horse. As you do ...

In 1693 Ragusa had to be largely rebuilt following a devastating earthquake, which killed some 5,000 inhabitants. The rebuilding - in the 17th and 18th centuries when Sicily was part of the Spanish Empire - allowed the Sicilian Baroque style to come to prominence. The style is recognisable not only by its typical Baroque curves and flourishes, but also by its grinning masks and putti and a "particular flamboyance that has given Sicily a unique architectural identity".

Featured sites include the Cathedral of San Giorgio with its Catalan-Gothic style portal (and how odd to see the Patron Saint of England - St George - being claimed by another country) and the tranquil Giardini Iblei.

And a beautiful place to stop for lunch - Agua Beach Resort.
