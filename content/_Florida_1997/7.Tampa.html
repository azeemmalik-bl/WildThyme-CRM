Tampa

Arriving in Tampa, we visited the historic Tampa Film Theatre and watched Atom Egoyan's The Sweet Hereafter with Ian Holm, an art film unusual for a venue such as this.

 You can't beat a straight-out bonkers keyboard player. Tampa's very own female version of Doctor John (albeit it only playing in a car park) ...
 

Created in 1947 by stunt swimmer and attraction promoter Newt Perry, who based the show on underwater air-hose breathing techniques, "Weeki Wachee Springs" was first named by Seminole Indians, which means 'Little Spring' or 'Winding River' in their language.

 
First an 18-seat theatre, then later a newer theatre with a capacity of 50, which was embedded in the lime rock of the spring with viewing windows below the surface of the water, to allow visitors to watch the mermaids perform in the water.
 
The brown pelican (Pelecanus occidentalis) is a North American bird of the pelican family, Pelecanidae. It is one of three pelican species found in the Americas and one of two that feed by diving in water. It is found on the Atlantic Coast from Nova Scotia to the mouth of the Amazon River, and along the Pacific Coast from British Columbia to northern Chile, including the Galapagos Islands.
