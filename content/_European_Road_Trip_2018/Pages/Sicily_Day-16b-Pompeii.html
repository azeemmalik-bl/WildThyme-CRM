Day 16 - 10 May 2018 (Pompeii)
     
 

Despite my flippancy elsewhere, it’s a tragic story. It is believed that about 2,000 people died in Pompeii when Vesuvius erupted in AD.79, yet only 1,150 individuals have been discovered since excavations began in the mid-19th century. Pompeii was buried under 8 to 9 feet of material and bodies (including those of dogs) were encased in layers of hardened pumice and ash.A
