Day 29 - 23 May 2018 (Siena)
     
 

According to legend, Siena is named after Senius (son of Remus) and therefore nephew of Romulus (after whom Rome was named). I feel almost that old as I realise the last time I was in Siena was at the end of my first year at university in 1979 - which makes it 39 years ago. And you wonder why I’ve turned to drink .. !!

The last time I was here was just a few days before the Palio (a bareback horse race which is held twice a year - on 2 July and 16 August) so the Piazza del Campo was full of workmen erecting barricades. This time we could see it in its full glory. For pub quiz fanatics, the Palio itself is run for three laps of the Piazza and usually lasts for no more than 90 seconds.
