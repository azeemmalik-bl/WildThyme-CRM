A cold grey Kensington roof is remodelled in the style of the Jardin Majorelle in Marrakech.

Mirrors open up the space and reflect the Yves Klein blue-painted trellis giving an impression of depth and space.

   

           
Planting is lush and exuberant - Hydrangea, Rosa 'Rhapsody in Blue', Clematis, Salvia, Cotinus, Osteospermum and Phygelius all create an intoxicating effect - and a heavenly scent.
