Isla de Flores

It was on the Isla de Flores on the shore of Lake Petén Itzá, that the last independent Maya state held out against the Spanish conquerors. In 1541, Hernán Cortés came to the island, en route to Honduras, but needed to move on and so did not stop to conquer it. The Spanish called the island, Tayasal, possibly derived from ta Itza, or "Place of the Itza".

It is now a pretty location for tourists with gaily-coloured buildings painted in every colour of the rainbow.

 
   
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 

Outside the funny little church was a statue of someone who looked remarkably like Fred Astaire or perhaps it was Desi Arnaz. The inscription gave nothing away so we were left none the wiser.

 
The town Christmas tree was already up, decorated and clearly sponsored by Gallo - the local beer (and it was only the beginning of November).
 
Back at the hotel we posed for photos overlooking the lake and then enjoyed a leisurely drink watching the sun go down.
