Day 34 - 28 May 2018 (Tournon-sur-Rhône)

In Tournon we find a mystery statue of a Marc Seguin. On further investigation we discover he is France’s answer to Brunel: inventor of the wire-cable suspension bridge and the multi-tubular steam-engine boiler (no less!). His mother was niece to one of the Montgolfier balloon pioneers. Here, to celebrate, are Seán and Sammy crossing the Rhône on his bridge.
