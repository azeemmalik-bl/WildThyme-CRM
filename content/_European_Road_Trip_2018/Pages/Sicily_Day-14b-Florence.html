Day 14 - 8 May 2018 (Florence)
     
 

Sammy keeps his distance from a nun, tries not to look like a tourist but spoils it by looking at the tourist garland of padlocks around the statue of Benvenuto Cellini on the Ponte Vecchio.

And then we had lunch today at a lovely rustic Tuscan place (La Vecchia Bettola) which was full of locals. Joining us at our outside table were two women who probably earned their living at night. A white van pulled up and the rough looking driver lunged into one of the diners with clearly a vendetta to settle. He was wrestled to the ground by a group of men. He then drove off. A few minutes later he returned on foot - this time with a knife - and launched himself at the diner again. A mega scuffle took place. The two women next to us took photos of the event! One of them gave Sammy four chunks of her fillet steak that she was unable to eat. As Seán stood to leave he was unable to stand as the metal chair had attached itself to the belt loop on his trousers and he had to be unhooked by a hooker!

Asparagus risotto!
