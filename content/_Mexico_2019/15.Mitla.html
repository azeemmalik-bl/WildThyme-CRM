Mitla
The ruins of ancient Mitla date from the three centuries before the Spanish conquest in the 1520s and were a cult centre dominated by High Priests who performed literally heart-wrenching human sacrifices. The Sala de las Columnas is 38m-long with six massive pillars, leading off this is El Palacio which features fabulous geometric mosaics which symbolise sky, earth, snakes and birds.
 
 
 
The Templo Católico de San Pablo Villa de Mitla is set in the midst of this Zapotec settlement in a pretty cactus garden. We entered the church at the same time as a child's funeral was taking place.
