20 - Checkmate

Written by Harley Peyton

Directed by Todd Holland

Original Airdate: 19 January 1991

“ My husband died in a fire. No one can know my sorrow. My love is gone. Yet, I feel him near me. Sometimes I can almost see him. At night when the wind blows, I think of what he might have been. Again I wonder: why?

“ When I see a fire, I feel my anger rising. This was not a friendly fire. This was not a forest fire. It was a fire *in the woods*. This is all I am permitted to say. ”

  • Following Major Briggs' mysterious reappearance, Cooper and Truman are frustrated as they question him to learn his whereabouts during his absence. He appears to have three triangular scars below his right ear and all he remembers is a disturbing image of a giant owl.
  • Cooper assists DEA Bryson and Truman with setting up Ernie Niles to arrest Jean Renault and Mountie King at Dead Dog Farm. But the sting goes bad due to Niles’ nervousness in which a stand-off ensues. Cooper then trades himself for Bryson and Niles and is held at gunpoint.
  • Dick Tremayne persuades Deputy Andy to help investigate Little Nicky's past by visiting the Dorritt Home for Boys orphanage.
  • Bobby has another argument with Shelly about taking care of the catatonic Leo, who does not remain that way for long
  • James Hurley succumbs to Evelyn Marsh's plea for help and continues romancing Evelyn while being observed by her chauffeur / brother Malcolm.
  • Mike Nelson continues being pursued by Nadine.
  • Harry visits Josie (now working as Catherine's maid) and discovers how afraid she is.
  • At the Great Northern Hotel, Audrey sees her father slipping further into insanity by re-enacting the Civil War in his office and thinking that he is General Robert E. Lee. A visit from an old lover interrupts him.
  • Donna misses James and discovers his whereabouts from Big Ed. She will take his savings money to him.
  • Meanwhile, Hank finally discovers the romantic relationship between Norma and Big Ed and, when he attempts to beat up Ed, he gets attacked and pulverized by the super-strong Nadine.
  • Audrey asks Bobby for help with her father's condition.
  • DEA Bryson brings food to the stand-off at Dead Dog Farm. Cooper grabs a gun she has concealed in her stockings and shoots Jean Renault.
  • Cooper's nemesis Windom Earle makes a horrifying opening move.

Unanswered Questions

  • Why is Major Briggs' work 'classified'?
  • What does Windom Earle intend?
