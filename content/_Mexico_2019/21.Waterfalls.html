Waterfalls

Just 20km south of Palenque, the spectacular Misol-Ha waterfall cascades approximately 35m into a wonderful wide pool surrounded by lush tropical vegetation. It is a sublime place for a dip when the waterfall is not excessively pumped-up by wet season rains. This was the location for the Arnold Schwarzeneggar film Predator.

 
 
A path behind the main fall leads into a cave which allows you to experience of the water close up - if you don't mind getting wet.

We then visited the Roberto Barrios waterfalls which tumble over a series of limestone hills and form a number of natural pools. We also saw a wide variety of beautiful butterflies and some extraordinary fungae.
