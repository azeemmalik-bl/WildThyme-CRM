Day 9 - 3 May 2018 (Lugano)

Built between 1905-1912, the Monte Brè funicular railway is comprised of two sections, lower and upper, with passengers having to change carriages half way. Cars to the lower section were replaced in 1959 and the upper (steeper) section in 1984 (thank goodness!). The summit is 933m above sea level, giving stunning views over Lugano and into Italy. There is also a charming restaurant with a panoramic terrace serving veal sausages (and chips) and Sammy had to be restrained from launching himself off in the manner of Icarus.
