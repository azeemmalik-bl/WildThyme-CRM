Rotorua

Pronounced "fa-ka-re-wa-re-wa", Whakarewarewa is a living village where the local Maori people still live as they have since the area was first settled in the 14th century. Here you can learn about Maori traditions such as flax weaving, bone carving and ta moko (tattooing). There are also performances although these, despite being performed seriously, seem to trivialise their age-old cultural traditions and even have a whiff of racial stereotyping about them.
