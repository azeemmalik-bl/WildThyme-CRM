Udaipur

.

Our accommodation in Udaipur was the scenic Amet Haveli, another 350-year old building which this time was on the lakeshore overlooking Udaipur City Palace and the luxurious Lake Palace Hotel. We enjoyed a late lunch while bathers dived off Hanuman Ghat.

 
It was a wonderful location and it felt more Mediterranean than Indian, that is until the bathers started hawking and clearing out their nasal passages ...
Later, from the City Palace, it was possible to look back over our accommodation (Amet Haveli), the low white building in the centre of the photo below.
After our lunch we crossed the bridge at the head of the lake and wandered around the old town. Seán made friends with a tame bullock and calf.
In a cafe where we stopped for a drink we were not unduly shocked by the filth of the toilet (we were used to this by now) but were astonished by the branding on it ...
 
We ended up in the atmospheric Savage Garden for a beer and supper and from where we enjoyed fabulous views of the sunset over Udaipur Lake.
