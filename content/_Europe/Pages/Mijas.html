Mijas - September 2013
     
 
 

Our friend, Rose from Nenagh in Ireland, was kind enough to let us stay in her lovely apartment on the edge of a golfcourse at Mijas (near Málaga) in Spain. It had a fabulous pool and great views over the local mountains.

In the mountain village of Mijas, we found what became a favourite restaurant of ours and to which we have returned on each subsequent trip. The food is simple but always delicious.

 

 

September is Seán's birthday month, and Rose and one of Sean's best friends (Geraldine) surprised him by flying out to the apartment to see him. We went into Fuengirola for drinks and a slap-up supper.

 

A quick word with the waiter and a surprise birthday cake appeared ...

 

The next day we went to the beach - another place to which we return every time we go to Rose's apartment. It has a super beach bar where we can get lunch and cold beers to keep us cool.

 

After a hard day at the beach, it was time for another trip into Fuengirola for drinks at the local Irish bar - where else?

 
The next day Rose and Geraldine had to fly back to Ireland. We returned to the beach after seeing them off safely.

 

On our final evening there was a magnificent sunset.
     
Mijas - September 2015
     
 
It was a delight to return to the apartment two years later where we found it as lovely as before.
 
 
Up to Mijas to our favourite restaurant again. These (below) were lightly fried zuccini with a honey and balsamic glaze. Then a seafood paella and grilled hake.
 
A truck which made us laugh as 'Bimbo' clearly means something different in Spanish ...
One day we drove into Málaga to visit the Cathedral which was constructed between 1528 and 1782, following the Renaissance-style plans drawn by Diego de Siloe. The north tower is 84 metres (276 ft) high, making this building the second-highest cathedral in Andalusia, after the Giralda of Seville. The south tower remains unfinished. To defray the enormous expenses of the work the Crown, after the War of Succession, imposed an excise duty or tax on all ships that called in Málaga.
 
 
 
The rather mawkish Beheading of Saint Paul was painted by Enrique Simonet in 1887.
 
 
Málaga is home to the Picasso Museum which holds some fantastic works of his.
 
Paul on a Donkey (1923) and Olga, Portrait of a Woman (1923)
 
Portrait of Dora Maar (1943) and Seated Figure (1971)
 
When we visited, the Picasso Museum was holding an exhibition of sculptures by French-born American sculptor Louise Bourgeois which I had seen when it had been shown at Tate Modern in London. The hot sunny climate in Málaga gave them a completely different emphasis. Above are Spider (1994-1999) and The Couple (2003).
Another day, another beach!
 
 
Another year, another birthday!
 
Near Rose's apartment is an outlet which sells ornaments and columns for gardens and installations. These fingers take us by surprise every time we see them! But who on earth would put them in their garden?
 
We found some party hats in a cupboard so decided to "party in pink" !
 
     
Mijas - September 2017
     
Another two years whizzed by before we were able to return to 'Las Golondrinas'.
A wide variety of holiday reading for the week, while Seán makes a delicious tortilla for lunch.
Up to Mijas to our favourite restaurant again (we both had the same this time) and then a walk around the village found some locals who had come in on horseback.
 
A bank window sums up Andalusia perfectly: religion, charcuterie and salsa lessons !
 
 
 
This year we drove into Marbella on a Sunday and found it fairly deserted but very hot. These street sculptures (above) were by Salvador Dalí.
 
A nice place for ice cream in the shade while we listened to a service through the open doors of the local church, the Iglesia Mayor de la Encarnacion.
 
 
 
It was a great shame that the local macramé shop was closed on a Sunday, otherwise we might have bought up all the stock ...!
 
Delicious meals eaten on the terrace and, bottom right, at the local roadside café.
 
A final day lazing on our favourite beach.
 
The 'finger store' was still selling their digits - we wondered if you had to buy them in sets of five! Our last evening with warm breezes and a beautiful sunset.
