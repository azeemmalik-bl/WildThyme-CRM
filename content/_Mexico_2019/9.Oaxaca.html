Oaxaca
Amazing floral displays in the main Pantheon Cemetery at Oaxaca. It was a fabulous emotional (but surprisingly happy) experience. Whole families sat round graves eating and drinking and introducing young children to the memory of their forebears. It seemed so much more healthy and cleansing to do it this way than the very buttoned-up way we deal with death back in England.
 
 
Exuberant marimba playing with a riot of flowers and colours. It’s just a shame you can’t smell the intoxicating scents of flowers, incense and the cooking of empanadas ...
