Vietnam - Bên Tre
     
 

Delightful 82 year old gent who served us fruit on our wanderings through the backwaters yesterday. He tells us he has 8 children and 13 grandchildren. He must have lived through a heck of a lot of history here in Vietnam.

We also stopped at a sweatshop where they were making snazzy football shoes for Nike. The temperature was boiling in the workshop (look how frazzled I look) and the workers were probably paid a pittance to make the shoes which are then sold expensively back home.

Plus some photos of our home stay location and our dinner, fried elephant ear fish (gourami). It's always nice to meet your dinner before you eat them ...
