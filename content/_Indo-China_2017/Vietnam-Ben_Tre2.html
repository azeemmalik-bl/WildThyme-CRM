Vietnam - Bên Tre
     
 

Viewers with a delicate disposition, look away now ...

Looking more like a medieval charnel house, these offal and meat stalls in Bên Tre market would not look out of place in a slasher movie. I've never felt more like converting to vegetarianism (and you know that really means something to a confirmed carnivore like me). The final touch was the Madame Defarge-like creature sleeping in her hammock above the slabs of meat below.

Plus a surprise cake on the eve of my birthday ...
