FILMING LOCATIONS

Holdenby House near Northampton was used by the BBC for the exterior set of Satis House while the interior scenes were filmed on location in Langleybury Mansion, a former country house at Kings Langley, near Watford, Hertfordshire.

The Gargery House/Forge was built on marshland to the east of the Essex village of Tollesbury, whilst the village church seen in the opening scenes of the series was the St Thomas a Beckett church, located in the deserted Kent village of Fairfield on the Romney Marsh.

Scenes for the interior of the Gargery House/Forge were filmed at Luton Hoo Farm in Bedfordshire and the interiors of Pip's Club were filmed at the Luton Hoo Hotel.
