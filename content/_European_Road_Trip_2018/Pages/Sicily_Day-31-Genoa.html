Day 31 - 25 May 2018 (Genoa)
     
 

Birthplace of Christopher Columbus, Niccolò Paganini, Giuseppe Mazzini and Renzo Piano, Genoa is the sixth largest city and the busiest port in Italy. I was last here about 25 years ago having had a delicious Sunday lunch at a fairly expensive restaurant in a smart square only to discover, when the bill arrived, that they didn’t take credit cards and the nearby ATMs didn’t work as it was Ascension Day or some such ridiculous Italian reason. After a two hour nail-biting wait (drinking more wine and yet more coffee) computer lines were restored and I could finally withdraw enough cash to settle the bill.

No such dramas this time and lunch comprised of a simple, but delicious, Pasta al Portofino (with home-made basil pesto and the sweetest ciliegini) in the shade of Chiesa S. Maria della Vigne which, frankly, was decorated like a tart’s boudoir.

And, talking of tarts, we were surprised so many street walkers working in the quaint streets around the UNESCO world heritage buildings in via Garibaldi ...
