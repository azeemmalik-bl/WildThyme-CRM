Abhaneri

.

This remote village is an unlikely location for one of Rajasthan's most awe-inspiring wells, the Chand Baori. With around 11 visible levels of zigzagging steps, this 10th century water tank is an incredible geometric sight, 20m deep. Flanking the baori is a small, crumbling palace where the royals used to bathe in private rooms - it's now inhabited by pigeons and bats.

It is thought to have been built by King Chand, ruler of Abhaneri and a Rajput from the Chahmana dynasty.
