Disneyland
 

Disneyland was the first of two theme parks built in Anaheim, CA and opened on 17 July 1955. It is the only theme park designed and built to completion under the direct supervision of Walt Disney. He came up with the concept of Disneyland after visiting various amusement parks with his daughters in the 1930s and 1940s. He initially envisioned a tourist attraction adjacent to his studios in Burbank to entertain fans who wished to visit, but soon realised that the proposed site was too small and so bought the 160 acre site near Anaheim in 1953.

Since its opening, Disneyland has undergone expansions and major renovations, including the addition of New Orleans Square in 1966, Bear Country (now Critter Country) in 1972, Mickey's Toontown in 1993 and Star Wars: Galaxy's Edge in 2019.
