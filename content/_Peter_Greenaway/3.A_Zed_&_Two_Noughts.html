A ZED & TWO NOUGHTS
1985
         
 

This film was Greenaway's first collaboration with cinematographer Sacha Vierny (who worked with Alain Resnais on Hiroshima mon amour and L'année dernière à Marienbad, and Luis Buñuel on Belle de Jour, who went on to shoot virtually all of Greenaway's work in the 1980s and 1990s, until Vierny's death. Greenaway referred to Vierny as his "most important collaborator". Filmed at Rotterdam Zoo, the film is a rumination on life, love, sex, doubling, man's mistreatment of animals, artifice versus the life force and the inevitability of birth, death and decay.

Elements of Michael Nyman's score invoke the "Dies Irae" section from Heinrich Ignaz Franz Biber's Requiem ex F con terza minore. The "Angelfish Decay"/ "Swan Rot"/ "L'Escargot" theme was originally written for Childs Play, a dance work commissioned by Lucinda Childs.

Twin zoologists, Oswald and Oliver Deuce (Brian Deacon and Eric Deacon), are at work studying the behaviour of animals at a zoo when both their wives are killed in a car accident involving a large swan which crashes through the car windscreen. The woman who was driving the car, Alba Bewick (Andréa Ferréol), is not killed, but has a leg amputated. Venus de Milo (Frances Barber), a prostitute and storyteller who plies her trade at the zoo, attempts to forge a relationship with the twins, ostensibly to help them recover from their loss. Meanwhile, Oswald and Oliver gradually become obsessed with images of growth and decay, watching videos on the origins of life and creating time-lapse video of decomposing life forms. They begin this latter task with a green apple, bitten into and rotting before their camera lens ...
