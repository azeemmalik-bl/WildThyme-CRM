This garden was overgrown and full of large dull plants. The client wanted to open it up and use it as an outdoor dining room. A wooden Ip'e deck was installed with integral electric LED lighting around the edge to define the area and the beds filled with bold foliage and jungle planting. Candles and lanterns are also included in the planting to enhance its night-time use.
