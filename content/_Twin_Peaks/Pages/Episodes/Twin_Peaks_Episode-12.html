12 - The Orchid's Curse

Written by Barry Pullman

Directed by Graeme Clifford

Original Airdate: 27 October 1990
       

" Sometimes nature plays tricks on us and we imagine we are something other than what we truly are. Is this a key to life in general? Or the case of the two-headed schizophrenic? Both heads thought the other was following itself. Finally, when one head wasn't looking, the other shot the other right between the eyes, and, of course, killed himself. "

 

• Cooper finally finds Audrey's message which she slipped under his door and figures out that she is being held captive at One-Eyed Jack’s and asks Truman to help him sneak into the place to rescue her.

• Bobby and Shelly plan making over her house to assist Leo's return to take bed-care of him, while Mr. Pinkle gets stuck in a mechanical device showing them how to work it.

• Leland Palmer's court hearing is held at the Roadhouse where Judge Sternwood releases him on his own will.

• Judge Sternwood also rules that Leo Johnson is not competent to stand trial for the sawmill fire.

• Hank is ordered by Ben Horne to follow Cooper to make sure he delivers the ransom money and get Audrey back.

• Big Ed brings the crazy and delusional Nadine home from the hospital.

• The mysterious Japanese businessman, Tojamura, approaches Ben Horne and offers him a $5 million cheque for the purchase of Ghostwood Estates.

• Andy, covering for Lucy desk job while she's visiting her sister in Tacoma, learns that he is not sterile after all.

• Blackie is murdered, like Battis, by Jean Renault when her usefulness runs out.

• At One-Eyed Jack’s, Jean Renault plots with Blackie's sister, Nancy, to steal the ransom money for themselves after it is delivered.

• Cooper and Truman escape with Audrey from One-Eyed Jack's after Hawk deals with an unwelcome bodyguard. They are seen by Hank who, in turn, is seen by Jean Renault.

• Donna enlists Maddy's help to help her steal Laura's secret diary away from Harold Smith who has become dangerously unstable and catches them in the act.

Unanswered Questions

• Will Harold Smith kill Donna and Maddy? It seems that Donna's betrayal of him as plunged him into madness. What is he capable of?

• Who is Tojamura? (See Lonely Souls).

• How did Hawk know that Cooper and Truman would be going to One-Eyed Jack’s? His excuse was pretty feeble.
