Road to Milford Sound
     
 

Today we drove to Milford Sound via the spectacular Mirror Lakes which perfectly reflect the images of the mountains across the valley. We stopped at Knob's Creek and saw the cheekiest birds I have encountered (Kea - Alpine Parrots) which hang around and climb all over tourists' cars looking for food. Then a dash through the Homer Tunnel with the world's most alpine set of traffic lights to direct traffic.
