Ventimiglia - August 1993
     
 
 

A return trip to Ventimiglia, this time in August 1993, with friends Ian and Danny.  As you can see, we flew from Stansted airport.
 

Back in Manuela's apartment, everything was comfortingly the same, even if the residents this time were different and moodier.
 
The morning routine of baking croissants remained consistent too.
As my spoken Italian was more-or-less non-existent, Ian insisted on giving me a lesson in pronunciation which seemed to involve me clenching my teeth ...
 
 
And then to the beach which was only five minutes away - and there were several really nice restaurants along the sea-front, which we had to try (naturally) ...
 
A bus trip up into the hills took us to the pretty village of Dolceacqua.
 
 
 
 
After a heavy day sight-seeing, it was back down to the beach for another swim before supper.
 
 
 
 
Ian had to return early to England, so we spent his last morning having a mooch around 'old' Ventimiglia and then a final lunch.
 
While in the 'old' town we noticed posters for 'Medieval Ventimiglia' events which were to commence that evening.
 
We returned in the evening to see the historical procession of the Sestieri - rival groups and clans all dressed in brightly-coloured tunics and carrying matching flags which they whirled around and threw to each other in a spectacular candle-lit display.
Danny and I then took a train trip to Genoa - to see the Cathedral and tour the city.
 
 
English National Opera were performing Verdi's Simon Boccanegra later that season and I managed the singer who would portray the title role, so it was particularly pleasing to track down some proof that the character actually existed.
 
 
It was Ascension Day in Italy and all banks were closed. The restaurant where we had lunch did not take credit cards and we were in a bit of a pickle as we didn't have enough cash to pay the bill. Eventually, after a nerve-wracking two hours, the ATM nearby finally chugged into life and spat out some cash at us - hence Danny's excitement ...
 
Chiesa S. Maria della Vigne.
 
 
 
 
Our final day in Ventimiglia before we had to return to Nice Airport to catch the plane home.
