Cambodia - Phnom Penh

A stressful crossing from Vietnam over into Cambodia: our guide had to bribe the Vietnam border officials to process our exit visas in a 'timely fashion', we then had to trudge about a quarter of a mile in the blazing sun across No-Man's-Land overlooked by watch towers while dragging our suitcases and then, at the Cambodia border had to pay a further 'bribe' to enter the nation.

Phnom Penh means the hill (phnom) of Lady Penh. Legend has it that in 1373 she found four Buddha statues floating in the Mekong and built this hill and pagoda to worship them. She was successful and now has all number of rather fetching (albeit camp) statues of various deities. Note especially the offerings of a stuffed pig's head, fresh meat placed in the mouths of the temple guardians and some enchanting temple maidens selling lotus flowers.
