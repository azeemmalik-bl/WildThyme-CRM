Guatemala

Our arrival into Guatemala was unusual and seemingly rather irregular - by two long-boats - with our luggage in a third.

 
 
A chaotic scene met us on the shores of Guatemala with dogs barking and people scrambling over each other to carry our luggage up the muddy path to a bus which was awaiting us.
Further chaos ensued with some obviously illegal money changers offering Guatemalan Quetzels for Mexican Pesos or US Dollars through the bus door and windows at ridiculously poor exchange rates. They all smiled, and no wonder at the money they were making ...
 
 
 
The bus set off down an interminably long road through biblical sounding settlements such as Bethel and Eben-Ezer and the road was frequently blocked by herds of cattle and the cowboys looking after them.
 
 
Finally we arrived in Flores where we were able to shop for food and other 'essentials' in a Guatemalan supermarket.
 
 
 
 
Back into the bus and onto the Isla de Flores where we could check-in to our (rather smart) hotel with pool and a fabulous view across Lake Petén Itzá. A welcome glass of watermelon juice and then we moved onto something more alcoholic - the local beer.
