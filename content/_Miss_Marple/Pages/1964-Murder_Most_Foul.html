1964 - Murder Most Foul
Murder Most Foul was based on Agatha Christie's 1952 Poirot novel Mrs. McGinty's Dead - and changed Hercule Poirot for Miss Marple as the detective.

The title is a quote from Hamlet (I.v.27-28), where the Ghost comments about his own death:

"Murder most foul as in the best it is / But this most foul, strange and unnatural."
 
             
 

Screen Versions
 

Directed by George Pollock, the story is ostensibly based on the original Christie work, but notably changes the action and characters. Hercule Poirot is replaced by Miss Marple and most other characters are not in the original story.
