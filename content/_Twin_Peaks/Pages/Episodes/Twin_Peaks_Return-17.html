17 - The Past Dictates The Future

Written by Mark Frost and David Lynch

Directed by David Lynch

Original Airdate: 3 September 2017
     
 

Gordon reveals a secret he has kept for 25 years - that, before his disappearance, Major Briggs shared with Gordon and Cooper a discovery he made about an extreme negative force named "Jowday", the name of which over time became "Judy". Phillip Jeffries also was aware of this entity.

Agent Headley calls Gordon to tell him that they have caught up with Dougie Jones - but found his hospital bed empty. Bushnell Mullins reads Cooper's coded message to Gordon, which reveals that Cooper is Dougie Jones. Tammy looks up Cooper's activities and Gordon says he knows where he is headed - Twin Peaks.

The remaining coordinates lead Cooper's doppelgänger to "Jack Rabbit's Palace". He is transported to the Fireman, then to the Sheriff's Department, and mistaken for the real Cooper.

While talking to the doppelgänger, Frank receives a call from the real Cooper. The doppelgänger draws a gun but, shockingly, is shot by Lucy.

Cooper, the FBI and the Mitchums arrive.

Woodsmen tear at the doppelgänger's body, releasing BOB's orb. Freddie knocks out Chad, who had escaped his cell, and smashes BOB's orb with several punches.

Cooper puts the Owl Cave ring on the doppelgänger's finger, sending him to the Black Lodge.

Naido transforms into the real Diane - this time with red hair.

Cooper unlocks a door at the Great Northern, where MIKE recites the "fire walk with me" verse and takes him to the convenience store where Cooper speaks with Jeffries about the date 23 February 1989. Jeffries gives Cooper instructions to find "Judy" and, out of the steam, an Owl Cave symbol turns into two diamonds and then into an 8.

Cooper is transported to the night Laura died and prevents Laura's murder; in the morning, her corpse vanishes before it was ever found by Pete Martell.

As he leads her through the woods, she disappears.

Sarah screams and smashes Laura's photo.
