Day 22 - 16 May 2018 (Noto)
     
 

According to legend, Daedalus stayed in the city of Noto after his flight over the Ionian Sea, as did Hercules after his seventh task. In 866 it was conquered by the Arabs but, in 1091, it became the last Islamic stronghold in Sicily to fall to the Christians. Later it became a rich Norman city. Noto was freed from the fascist dictatorship of Mussolini in July 1943 and the Notinesi people voted in favour of the monarchy in the referendum of 1946.
