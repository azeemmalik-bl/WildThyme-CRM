Norway & Fjords - 2019

A seven night cruise on the 2,700 capacity Sapphire Princess to Norway visiting Bergen, beautiful fjords (Geiranger, Hellesylt, Olden) and Stavanger.

 
 

Departure was from Southampton on a cool overcast day.

 
 

A quick tour of the boat took us from the glitzy atrium complete with white concert grand piano to the outdoor movie screen ("Cinema under the stars") where some passengers had to wrap themselves in blankets to keep warm ...
