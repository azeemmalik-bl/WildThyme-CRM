Plot

A character study of a well-kept, upscale Manhattan woman (Mia Farrow) takes the title character on a journey through a Wonderland of her own making, in which she learns some truths about herself, her relationships, and the universe in general. Alice leads a comfortable life, except for some nagging aches and pains, but when she visits the mysterious Dr. Yang (Keye Luke), he discovers that what really ails Alice is her own lack of true human experience.

Alice has been married for sixteen years to Doug (William Hurt), an emotionally detached stockbroker, and she lives a perfectly maintained life in a perfectly maintained apartment, with a pair of children and the requisite support staff. All that changes when a chance meeting with a neighbour (Joe Mantegna) leads Alice to consider an affair. Dr. Yang, seizing the opportunity, gives Alice herbal potions that make her both invisible and seductive, allowing her to free herself from her inhibitions. Plunging into her new fantasy world, Alice ultimately comes to terms with her family, her husband, and her life.

Filming

This was the first of two Woody Allen films in which Joe Mantegna and Judy Davis played a married couple. The second was Celebrity (1998).

This was Keye Luke's final film before his death on 12 January 1991 at the age of 86. He died less than three weeks after the film's release.

Sean Young filmed a small role but it was later cut. She had also been deleted from Woody Allen's previous film Crimes and Misdemeanors (1989).

When Thelonious Monk's version of Darn That Dream appears on the soundtrack, the LP sleeve of Monk's Dream is shown, implying that Alice and Joe are listening to it. However the tune is not featured on that album.
