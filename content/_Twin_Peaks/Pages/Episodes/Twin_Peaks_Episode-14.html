14 - Lonely Souls

Written by Mark Frost

Directed by David Lynch

Original Airdate: 10 November 1990
       

" A poem as lovely as a tree:

As the night wind blows, the boughs move to and fro. The rustling, the magic rustling that brings on the dark dream. The dream of suffering and pain. Pain for the victim, pain for the inflictor of pain. A circle of pain, a circle of suffering. Woe to the ones who behold the pale horse. "

 

• Cooper, Truman, and Cole take the One-Armed Man to the Great Northern Hotel, which is hosting a USO stop-over, in their search for BOB where MIKE shrieks and panics when Ben Horne approaches.

• Audrey tells her father that she knows about Laura's employment at One-Eyed Jack’s, and she tells Cooper about Ben's involvement whereupon Cooper and Truman arrest him as a suspect in Laura's murder.

• Hawk searches Harold Smith's house for Laura Palmer's secret diary only to find Harold dead from a suicide, and his house and Laura's diary torn apart by him. His last note was J'ai une âme solitaire, which is French for I have a lonely soul. These are the same words that Mrs. Tremond's grandson (the Creamed Corn Kid) said to Donna.

• Finances strain the relationship between Shelly and Bobby having misjudged the money due them.

• Leland Palmer tries to discourage Maddy from going home.

• Meanwhile, Shelly tells Norma that she's quitting working at the Double-R Diner to care for Leo.

• Bobby and Mike find a mini-cassette in Leo's boots.

• Tojamura plans to buy the Ghostwood Estates and, when he learns of Ben's arrest, goes to the Blue Pines B&B and surprises Pete Martell by revealing that 'he' is actually the missing Catherine Martell in disguise.

• The Log Lady knows that something was going to happen at the Roadhouse, where Cooper saw the Giant.

• In the climatic twist at the Roadhouse, Cooper receives another message from the Giant who knows that BOB is killing Maddy across town and tells Cooper that "It is happening again".

• Maddy is alerted by the vile smell of burning oil and tragically learns the identity of Laura's killer and who BOB inhabits.

• Leland Palmer, possessed by BOB, drugs Sarah and then murders Maddy and inserts a capital "O" under her fingernail.

• Leland is, and always has been, the killer.

Unanswered Questions

• How much of Leland's actions have been Leland and what has been BOB?

• Does Leland know what BOB is doing?

• Does Leland have a hope of ridding himself of BOB?

• What is on the cassette which Bobby found in Leo's boots?

• Where has Catherine been? How did she survive?

• Who is the Giant? Why does he keep leaving cryptic clues for Cooper?

• BOB seemed under the impression that Maddy was Laura during the murder. Does this mean that he killed her simply to relive the experience of the first murder? If the burning oil smell accompanies BOB's appearance, does that mean that Leland was under the influence of BOB when he attacked Jacoby, as Jacoby sensed the same smell at the Gazebo?
