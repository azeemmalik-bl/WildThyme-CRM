Singapore

The obligatory stop at Raffles Hotel and a tour round its pretty garden with the delicious scent of the frangipani trees. Then a walk round the historic areas, along the now cleaned-up river (it used to stink when I lived in Singapore as a child), seeing the old General Post Office (now the Fullerton Hotel) and up North Bridge Road (the Indian quarter). We also met a pal from Philip's moustache club in London who was working in Singapore.
