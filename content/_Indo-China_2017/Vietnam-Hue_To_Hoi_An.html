Vietnam - Huê to Hoi An
     
 

At Ngu Hanh Son (Marble Mountain) which represents the five elements - water, wood, fire, gold and earth. The villages that have sprung up at the base of the mountains offer a range of hideous marble Buddhas, lions, fish, frogs and Jesuses although they now astutely use marble imported from China rather than wreck their own money-making mountain.

Danang's Dragon Bridge which spouts fire and water from the dragon's head on Saturday and Sunday nights (we missed it) and some scenes on China Beach where Seán and Dawn recreate the famous beach scene in From Here to Eternity.

Hilarious pre-wedding photos being taken in storm-force winds on a concrete bunker. The bride looks absolutely frozen. How romantic is that?
