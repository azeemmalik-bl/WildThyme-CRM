Day 27 - 21 May 2018 (Castel Gandolfo)

Castel Gandolfo, just outside Rome, is traditionally where the Pope goes to spend the summer, away from the hustle, bustle and heat of the Vatican. It is now a museum where you can see the collection of Pope-mobiles and previous cars which Popes have used. And none of them are Fiat Pandas ...
