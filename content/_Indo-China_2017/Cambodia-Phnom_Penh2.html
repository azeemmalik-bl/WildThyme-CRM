Cambodia - Phnom Penh

We also visited the stunning Royal Palace with its ornate gilding and flights-of-fancy roof lines. I felt I was starring in my very own version of The King and I. (And yes, I know that's set in Thailand).

The palace was constructed after King Norodom relocated the royal capital from Oudong to Phnom Penh in the mid-19th century. It was built atop an old citadel called Banteay Kev. It faces towards the East and is situated at the Western bank of the cross division of the Tonle Sap and Mekong Rivers.s.

Looking more like the original line-up of The Nolans, these mannequins display the daily dress colours of the ladies-in-waiting to the Queen. Male bodyguards are colour-coordinated too. I'm in the mood for dancing indeed ...
