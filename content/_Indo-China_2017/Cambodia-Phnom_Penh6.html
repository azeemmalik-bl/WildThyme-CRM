Cambodia - Phnom Penh

Phnom Penh's Central Market was constructed in 1937 in the Art Deco style. It is in the shape of a dome with four arms branching out into vast hallways with countless stalls of goods and was designed by French architect Jean Desbois (1891-1971). When it first opened in 1937, it was said to be the biggest market in Asia.

It has now been extensively renovated but the surrounding area is still shabby..

We were fascinated by the focus on "beauty" in the market. There are businesses here that export much of their supply of human hair to the African and Jewish markets. You won't recognise Philip with his hair extensions when you next see him.
