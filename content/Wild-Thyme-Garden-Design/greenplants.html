Equisetum hyemale

Seemingly THE designer plant of this season.
Its architectural spikes of emerald green are especially attractive when covered with frost in winter. The wild version (field horsetail) is a popular food item with grizzly bears!

Kniphofia 'Percy's Pride'

Not all red-hot pokers have to be "red hot" and vulgar. Delicate lime-green varieties are a fantastic foil to orange or raspberry-coloured flowers such as Knautia macedonica or Astrantia 'Garnet'.

 

 

 

Matteuccia struthiopteris

A low-maintenance hardy fern. Excellent for shady borders and damp sites. Beautiful in spring as the fronds unfurl.

Sasa veitchii

Called kumazasa in Japanese.
The leaves turn straw-coloured at the margins in autumn. It grows well in cool areas and, unlike tall bamboo, prefers the shade.

 

 

 

Euphorbia characias

A spectacular all-purpose plant
for the border which grows
very tall and provides excellent architectural shape.
