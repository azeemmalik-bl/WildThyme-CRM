Laos - Vientiane
     
 

Built between 1819 and 1824 by Chao Anou, Wat Si Saket is believed to be Vientiane's oldest surviving temple. And it shows: this beautiful temple (with its thousands of Buddha figures) is in dire need of a facelift.

We also met an artist selling his work. This man used to be an art teacher before the 'Vietnam War' which spilled over into Laos (Laos was secretly bombed by US forces under orders from Presidents Kennedy, Johnson and Nixon) forcing him to change his way of life.
