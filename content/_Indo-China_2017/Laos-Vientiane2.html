Laos - Vientiane
At Wat Si Muang. Our first visit to a temple - that of the Emerald Buddha. In the large sim (ordination hall) petitioners who can lift a stone copy of it from its pillow three times whilst kneeling are supposed to have their wishes granted. With our lovely travelling companion for the next three weeks - Joan Thomson - and our rather irritating guide Khamla Lorvanxay "La". Plus a gratuitous shot of some officials doing their exercises ...
