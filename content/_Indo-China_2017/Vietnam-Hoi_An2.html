Vietnam - Hoi An
     
 

Exquisite 18th century houses in Hoi An Historic Centre. Although the town is prone to flooding it is beautifully preserved and reeking with atmosphere.

There's nothing quite like a dusty old museum (and some awkwardly dressed mannequins) for a giggle. No wonder he looks miserable: his wife has a broken arm and a moustache ...

The Vinh Hung Hotel features in the 2002 film A Quiet American featuring Michael Caine.

And on the Japanese Covered Bridge we found a shrine to Saint Sammy and his monkey friend.
