Moeraki Boulders

These stones are bizarre. Unusually large and spherical, they lie along a stretch of Koekohe Beach on the Otago coast between Moeraki and Hampden and occur either as stand alone or clusters of boulders. Their smooth skins hide honeycomb centres which are revealed in some of the broken specimens. They were originally formed around a central core of carbonate of lime crystals that attracted minerals from their surroundings - a process that started sixty million years ago. Now exhumed from the mudstone enclosing them they have been concentrated on the beach by coastal erosion.
