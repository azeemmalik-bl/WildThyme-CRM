International Pilot

Written by Mark Frost and David Lynch

Directed by David Lynch

 
       

" Welcome to Twin Peaks. My name is Margaret Lanterman. I live in Twin Peaks. I am known as the Log Lady. There is a story behind that. There are many stories in Twin Peaks - some of them are sad, some funny. Some of them are stories of madness, of violence. Some are ordinary. Yet they all have about them a sense of mystery - the mystery of life. Sometimes, the mystery of death. The mystery of the woods. The woods surrounding Twin Peaks.

" To introduce this story, let me just say it encompasses the all - it is beyond the 'fire', though few would know that meaning. It is a story of many, but begins with one - and I knew her.

" The one leading to the many is Laura Palmer. Laura is the one. "

• The International Pilot is a self-contained version of the episode that contains twenty minutes of extra footage that solves the murder. This version was filmed in case the show was not picked up as a series, and instead released as a television movie.

• It has an alternate ending which is only seen in the International Pilot, which contains all previous scenes seen in the original Pilot, with an alteration in the final one, plus the additional twenty minutes.

• Sarah Palmer screams as she remembers seeing a gray-haired man crouched at the foot of Laura's bed. Deputy Hawk sketches the picture of the man. The One-Armed Man phones Cooper to tell him that Laura's killer is at the hospital.

• The One-Armed Man meets Cooper, Harry and Andy in the morgue where Laura's corpse had been. He tells them that the light doesn't work because of the transformer and then recites the "Fire Walk With Me" chant. He explains that he and the killer were living among 'the people', above a convenience store. He then tells his name is MIKE and that the killer is BOB. He also tells Cooper that he was in the hospital because he was searching for BOB who sometimes likes to walk among the injured of the species and that he has been searching for him for over a year. He says that he too was touched by the devilish one with a tattoo on his left shoulder but that, after seeing the face of God, he cut his entire arm off. Two sketches are then shown to him and he recognises the second one sketched by Hawk as being BOB. He then tells the group that BOB is in the basement.

• Cooper and Harry confront Killer BOB in the basement while he is performing a ritual with twelve candles in a circle. BOB is disappointed by not seeing MIKE, and wants to sing with him again but nonetheless speaks to his absent partner, asking if he hears him and recounting a pursuit with someone that he caught with "his death bag". MIKE sneaks in the shadow of the door frame, unnoticed by anyone. BOB explains that the letters were going to spell his proper name "Robert". He gleefully admits to murdering Teresa Banks who had the "T" (and by extension Laura who had the "R") and vows to kill again. MIKE immediately bursts in and shoots BOB to death with his own gun, before suffering a heart attack and dying. The twelve candles are extinguished as Cooper says "make a wish".

• Twenty five years later, Cooper finds himself in a red room. Also there are a little man ("The Man From Another Place") and Laura Palmer, who whispers something into Cooper's ear.
