6 - Realization Time

Written by Harley Peyton

Directed by Caleb Deschanel

Original Airdate: 17 May 1990
       

" Beauty is in the eye of the beholder. Yet there are those who open many eyes. Eyes are the mirror of the soul, someone has said. So we look closely at the eyes to see the nature of the soul.

" Sometimes when we see the eyes-those horrible times when we see the eyes, eyes that ... that have no soul-then we know a darkness, then we wonder: where is the beauty? There is none if the eyes are soulless. "

 

 

• Agent Cooper lets the seductive Audrey down easy by having a night-long talk with her about life and love.

• Lucy continues to treat Deputy Andy coldly. She receives a phone call from a Dr. Stanicek.

• Audrey Horne, having taken a job at her father's department store, discovers that Emory Battis, the manager, is sending girls from the perfume counter to work at One-Eyed Jack's. Unknown to Cooper, Audrey goes undercover as a hostess there and persuades Blackie to hire her.

• Cooper and Bookhouse Boy Big Ed decide to pay a special visit to One-Eyed Jack's to find Jacques Renault who, having fled the country, now lives on the Canadian side of the border.

• Josie Packard shares her suspicions with Truman about Catherine and Ben's plan to burn the sawmill. That is why she took photos of them at the motel.

• However Catherine learns that both Ben Horne and Josie are planning to murder her.

• A wounded Leo, now a fugitive, learns about Jacques' mynah bird at the police station and plots to kill it.  Waldo is shot by Leo but not before its voice is recorded on Cooper's dictaphone implicating Leo.

• The Icelandic businessmen still have not signed the Ghostwood Estates business deal so Ben Horne gets Jerry to take them to One-Eyed Jack's to persuade them.

• Ben makes a call to Leo to execute the burning down of the sawmill. He then makes another call to Hank Jennings to murder Leo, as well as Catherine, when the arson job at the sawmill is complete.

• Dr. Jacoby receives a phone call from Maddy pretending to be the dead Laura Palmer which lures him away from his office so James and Donna can search it to find the missing cassette tape.

•Bobby plants a stash of cocaine on James' motorbike while he is inside.

Unanswered Questions

• Why is Lucy treating Deputy Andy so badly?

• Bobby Briggs watched "Laura" (Maddy) at the Gazebo, but someone else was watching both of them. Who?
