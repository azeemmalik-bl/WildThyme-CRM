Vietnam - Ho Chi Minh City

Stunning James Bond-style interiors at the Reunification Palace. Presumably paid for with US money, it was in fact designed by Paris-trained Vietnamese architect Ngo Viet Thu. It was commissioned by President Ngo Dinh Diem who was so unpopular that his own air force bombed the previous palace in 1962 in an attempt to kill him. This new palace was finished in 1966 (complete with bomb-proof bunker in the basement) but Diem did not get to see his dream house as he was assassinated by his own troops in 1963.

It's a stunning building nonetheless - a perfect example of mid-60s moderne design.
