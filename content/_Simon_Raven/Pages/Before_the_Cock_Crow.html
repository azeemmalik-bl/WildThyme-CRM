Plot Summary

The story opens with Lord Canteloupe's strange toast to "absent friends". His wife 'Baby' has recently died and Canteloupe has been left her retarded son, Lord Sarum of Old Sarum. The child is not his, but had been conceived by Major Fielding Gray. In Italy there is an illegitimate child with a legitimate claim to the estate, whom Canteloupe wants silenced.

The plot also sees young Marius Stern and his school friend, Tessa Malcolm, drawn into Milo Hedley's schemes and into a dramatic finale orchestrated by Raisley Conyngham, Milo's teacher.
