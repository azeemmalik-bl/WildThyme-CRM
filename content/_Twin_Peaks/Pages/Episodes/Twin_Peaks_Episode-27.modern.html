27 - The Path To The Black Lodge

Written by Harley Peyton and Robert Engels

Directed by Stephen Gyllenhaal

Original Airdate: 19 April 1991

“ There are clues everywhere – all around us. But the puzzle maker is clever. The clues, although surrounding us, are somehow mistaken for something else. And the something else – the *wrong* interpretation of the clues – we call our world. Our world is a magical smoke screen. How should we interpret the happy song of the meadowlark or the robust flavour of a wild strawberry? ”

  • Cooper and Truman try to figure out Windom Earle's next move following the discovery of the heavy metal youth's body in a large chess pawn.
  • Lucy decides to enter the Miss Twin Peaks Beauty Pageant as the prize money would be useful for the unborn baby. She tells Andy that she will choose who the father of the baby will be in 24 hours.
  • Wheeler searches for Audrey before he has to leave town for Brazil to take over his murdered partner's business, and leaves a letter for Audrey.
  • Ben Horne has a health check up from 'Doc' Hayward who warns him to stay away from his wife. Ben is determined to "do the right thing".
  • Donna Hayward finds an old scrapbook full of surprises including her birth certificate on which her father's name is not included.
  • Cooper and Truman continue to investigate the mystery of Owl Cave and learn from Major Briggs that Windom Earle's real reason for being in Twin Peaks is to gain entrance into the Black Lodge. Cooper realises that the "chess game" has been a "red herring".
  • Cooper contacts Donna, Shelly and Audrey and confides about Windom Earle and warns them to be careful; however the lovesick Cooper does not think about warning Annie who also decides to enter the Beauty Pageant.
  • Lana continues her plans to fix the beauty pageant in her favour.
  • Major Briggs is abducted by Earle and Leo who interrogate him for information to what the Owl Cave markings mean, and learn that they are an actual map to the Black Lodge.
  • Audrey gets Pete, who is still mourning the loss of Josie, to rush her to the airport to intercept Wheeler to whom she makes a major confession.
  • Catherine shows Andrew Packard the puzzle box, who manages to get it open to reveal a small silver box.
  • Cooper dances with Annie and has a vision of The Giant who appears to be mouthing the word "No".
  • In a clearing in the woods, BOB appears.

Unanswered Questions

  • How will Windom Earle find the Black Lodge now that he has the map?
  • What does The Giant's warning mean?
