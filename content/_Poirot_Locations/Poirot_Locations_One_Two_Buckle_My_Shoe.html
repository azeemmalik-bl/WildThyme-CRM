Poirot visits the Dentist in Harley Street.

Lichfield Court in Richmond, Surrey is used as the location for 'Gerda's flat'.

Freemasons' Hall in Covent Garden used as 'The Astoria Hotel'.  (Also used in 'How Does Your Garden Grow?', 'The Adventure of the Western Star', 'Mrs McGinty's Dead' and 'Murder on the Orient Express').

Poirot visits Somerset House to check Alistair Blunt's wedding certificate.

Shrub's Wood, Chalfont St. Giles is used as Blunt's country home. (Also used in 'The Double Clue').

Marylebone Town Hall is used as 'Blunt's Bank'.
