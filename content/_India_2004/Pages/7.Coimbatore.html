Coimbatore

Coimbatore is a large business and industrial city known as either the Manchester or Detroit of the south for its textile manufacturing and engineering goods. While it doesn't hold any particular attraction for tourists, Coimbatore is a convenient overnight stop (although we didn't - we just went direct to the train station) for tourists heading up to Ooty or other Nilgiri hill stations. !

.
We found a mass of stalls offering letter writing services either by hand or using a typewrite.  Many of the inhabitants or people passing through would appear to be illiterate - hence the services offered..
