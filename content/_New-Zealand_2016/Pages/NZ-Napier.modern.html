Napier

An earthquake in 1931 destroyed many of Napier's brick buildings. Frantic reconstruction between 1931 and 1933 allowed architects, then in the throes of the global Art Deco mania, to create new streets with a definite and cohesive style. Art Deco was cheap, safe (low-rise as falling stone columns and collapsing balconies had killed many during the earthquake) and, above all, contemporary. Residents wanted to make a fresh start.
