Day 12 - 6 May 2018 (Como)
     
 

Another day, another Funicolare. This one is Como-Brunate and it takes you up to Volta’s Lighthouse (ideal for “seightseeing” sic.). Alessandro Volta (1745-1827) was an Italian physicist who is credited as the inventor of the electrical battery and discoverer of methane (as any owner of a dog will testify). So eager was Volta with his inventions he earned the nick-name “ever ready” - and was honoured by having a whole brand of batteries named after him.
