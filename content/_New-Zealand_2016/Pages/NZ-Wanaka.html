Road to Wanaka

At Haast we tried whitebait patties made with baby fish and an egg mix. Served with (1) lemon and salt and (2) mint sauce and garlic salt they were surprisingly delicious.

A quick stop at Thunder Creek Falls where, amazingly, we bumped into someone we knew from Kingsbridge back home!

With overgrown valleys, unspoiled rivers and tumbling glaciers, the Wanaka region is crowned with the colossal Mount Aspiring (Tititea), at 9,957 feet the highest peak outside the Mount Cook region. Wanaka is located at the southern end of Lake Wanaka, just over 60 miles northeast of Queenstown.
