Day 5 - 29 April 2018 (Luxembourg)
     
 

A quick flit to Luxembourg (as we’ve never been before) finds us in a charming ‘olde worlde’ setting which has the second highest per capita income in the world (and don’t you just know it : you can’t move for Chanel, Rolex and Tara Jarmon boutiques).

In 963, Siegfried I of the Ardennes, a close relative of King Louis II of France and Emperor Otto the Great, acquired the feudal lands of Luxembourg. Siegfried built his castle, named Lucilinburhuc ("small castle"), on the Bock Fiels ("rock"). So there you are!

I tasted a local fast food (“Kniddelen”) which, even with bacon, tasted as bland as it looks ... Home now for asparagus risotto!
