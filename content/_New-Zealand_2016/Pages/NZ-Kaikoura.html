Kaikoura

In Maori legend, Kaikoura Peninsula was the seat where the demigod Maui placed his feet when he fished the North Island up from the depths of the sea. We travelled across beautiful countryside to reach Kaikoura which, until the 1980s, was a sleepy crayfishing town (Kai meaning 'food', koura meaning 'crayfish'). It's now a major centre for whale watching.
