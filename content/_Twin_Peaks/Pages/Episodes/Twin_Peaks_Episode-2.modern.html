2 - Zen, or The Skill To Catch A Killer

Written by David Lynch and Mark Frost

Directed by David Lynch

Original Airdate: 19 April 1990

Next →

“Sometime ideas, like men, jump up and say 'hello'. They introduce themselves, these ideas, with words. Are they words? These ideas speak so strangely.

All that we see in this world is based on someone's ideas. Some ideas are destructive, some are constructive. Some ideas can arrive in the form of a dream. I can say it again: some ideas arrive in the form of a dream.”

  • FBI Agent Dale Cooper has a strange dream about Tibet, in which he gained the theory of the "trial by rock throwing". This shows that he is prepared to use unorthodox methods if it will help him find the killer.
  • Cooper's dream has serious connotations as it appears to implicate Dr. Jacoby and Leo Johnson as the "J" in Laura's diary.
  • Benjamin Horne and his brother, Jerry, take a trip just over the Canadian border to One-Eyed Jack's, a brothel / casino. Ben Horne is obviously a regular customer there, as Blackie (the hostess) obviously knows him well.
  • Jerry Horne works for his brother, travelling around and doing most of Ben's preparation work. It is obvious why this relationship works; Ben is clearly the smarter of the two.
  • Donna Hayward and James Hurley pledge their love.
  • Leo Johnson holds Bobby Briggs at gunpoint.
  • Leo Johnson deals in drugs, and Bobby and Mike both use the drugs themselves as well as selling them on.

Unanswered Questions

  • Who was the person with Leo in the woods?
  • Cooper's rocks struck the bottle on the names of Dr. Jacoby and Leo Johnson. In what way does this implicate them?
  • If BOB really is the killer, then where is he? We have not seen him in Twin Peaks, unlike his former associate, 'Mike', whom Deputy Hawk saw at the hospital.
  • Can Donna's and James' love survive the gruelling inquest into Laura's death, or will it just bring back too many memories for them?
  • Since Ben Horne owns everything else in Twin Peaks, is it possible that he owns One-Eyed Jack's as well? (See May The Giant Be With You).
