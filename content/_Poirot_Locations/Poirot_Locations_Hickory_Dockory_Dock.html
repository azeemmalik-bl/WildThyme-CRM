Allen's Butchers on Mount Street, London W1 which Poirot and Japp visit at the beginning of the episode.

The Youth Hostel in Carter Lane, London EC4 (previously the St.Paul's Choir School) used as the 'Students' Lodgings'.

This entrance in Carter Lane, London EC4 was used as 'Hickory Road Station'.

The Wilkins Building at UCL opposite University College Hospital (Also used in 'Four and Twenty Blackbirds' and 'Five Little Pigs').

University College Hospital in Gower Street, London WC1 where Sir Arthur Stanley is taken for treatment.

The Flaxman Gallery outside the main library at University College London where Poirot interviews Sally Finch.

Brushfield Street, Spitalfields, London E1 was used as the location of 'Mr Nicoletis' Shop'.

Brompton Cemetary Chapel, London SW10 where the funeral of Sir Arthur Stanley takes place.
