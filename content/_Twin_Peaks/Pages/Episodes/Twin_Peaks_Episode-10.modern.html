10 - The Man Behind Glass

Written by Robert Engels

Directed by Lesli Linka Glatter

Original Airdate: 13 October 1990

Next →

"Letters are symbols. They are building blocks of words which form our languages. Languages help us communicate. Even with complicated languages used by intelligent people, misunderstanding is a common occurrence.

"We write things down sometimes: letters, words, hoping they will serve us and those with whom we wish to communicate. Letters and words, calling out for understanding."

  • Cooper and Agent Rosenfield are alerted when an attempt is made on Ronette Pulaski's life at the hospital. The letter "B" is found inserted under her finger nail. They now have an "R" (from Laura Palmer), a "B" and a "T" (from Teresa Banks whose body was found one year earlier).
  • Meanwhile, Donna meets with the reclusive Harold Smith, an orchid cultivator, whom she asks about Laura. He explains that Laura wanted him to contact Donna if anything ever happened to Laura as she would "ask a lot of questions".
  • Leland comes to the police station after seeing the police sketch of Killer BOB claiming to have known the mysterious man from his childhood. He remembers his name as Robertson and that he used to flick matches at him saying "You want to play with fire, little boy?".
  • Elsewhere, Lucy meets with the suave and vain Dick Tremayne (Designer Menswear - Horne's Department Store) and confides that she's pregnant from a brief fling they had and the baby may be his.
  • At One-Eyed Jack's, Audrey is held prisoner by Blackie and Emory whom are under the employment of Jean Renault who plans to extract a ransom from Benjamin Horne, as well as kill Cooper whom he blames for the deaths of his brothers Bernard and Jacques.
  • Phillip Michael Gerard, the one-armed man, while selling shoes to Agent Truman has a seizure after seeing the sketch of Killer BOB and emerges from the bathroom as his alter ego MIKE. He disappears but Cooper finds a syringe on the floor of the bathroom which reminds him of the Giant's third clue - "Without chemicals, he points".
  • The mysterious Asian man staying at the Great Northern Hotel continues to follow Cooper around.
  • At the hospital, Nadine Hurley awakens from her coma but, to Big Ed's bewilderment, she thinks she's 18 years old and in high school.
  • Nearby, Dr. Jacoby is hypnotised by Cooper to look back into that night when Jacques Renault was murdered and remembers seeing Leland carry out the murder and smelling engine oil.
  • Maddy is upset because people "keep confusing her with Laura".
  • Sheriff Truman and Cooper come to Leland's house to arrest him for the murder of Jacques Renault.
  • Also, Donna continues to become jealous over James hanging around Maddy and runs to Harold Smith for comfort. She finds a diary of Laura's at his house.

Unanswered Questions
