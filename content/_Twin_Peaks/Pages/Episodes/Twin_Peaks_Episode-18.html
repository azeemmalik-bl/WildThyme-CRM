18 - Masked Ball

Written by Barry Pullman

Directed by Duwayne Dunham

Original Airdate: 15 December 1990

Next →

"Is life like a game of chess? Are our present moves important for future success? I think so. We paint our future with every present brush stroke.

"Painting. Colours. Shapes. Textures. Composition. Repetition of shapes. Contrast. Let nature guide us. Nature is the great teacher. Who is the principal?

"Sometimes jokes are welcome. Like the one about the kid who said: 'I enjoyed school. It was just the principle of the thing.'"

  • Mrs. Briggs worries about her husband's disappearance after which Cooper and Truman investigate his research with the Air Force which involved in classified dealings with paranormal events which includes an unofficial search for places called the White Lodge and the Black Lodge.
  • Gordon Cole phones to offer Cooper his full support and advises "Let a smile be your umbrella".
  • Truman defends Cooper's activities at One-Eyed Jack's, which are under investigation by the FBI and DEA but Cooper refuses to offer a defense to Agent Hardy and instead confuses the IA agent with more mystical talk.
  • James Hurley stops in a small town several miles from Twin Peaks where a young wealthy heiress, named Evelyn Marsh, picks him up and offers him a job to fix her husband's car.
  • Dick Tremayne tries to train himself for parenthood when he takes a young, rambunctious orphan boy, named Little Nicky, under his wing to impress Lucy.
  • Later, a transvestite DEA agent, named Dennis (Denise) Bryson, arrives to investigate the drug charges against Cooper.
  • Back in Twin Peaks, Nadine tries to win Mike Nelson's affections with her super-human strength which instead nets the eyes of the school's wrestling coach.
  • Josie who has shown up on Truman's doorstep reveals to him that she has run away from her employer, a South African gangster named Thomas Eckhardt, who is now looking for her.
  • All of Twin Peaks gathers for the wedding of Doug Milford to a young gold digger, named Lana Budding.
  • Windom Earle makes a move by sending Cooper a note and a cassette tape.
  • Hank interrupts Ben Horne's reminiscences about the past with grim reality.
  • Ben Horne is further distraught after learning from Hank that Jean Renault has ousted him as the owner of One-Eyed Jack's and Hank is now under Renault's employment.
  • Andy kicks up his heels with DEA Agent Bryson at the wedding reception of Dougie Milford and his young bride.
  • When Truman refuses to help Josie, she goes to Catherine who agrees to help under her own conditions.
  • It is also revealed that Andrew Packard, whom Eckhardt hired Josie to kill and who later sub-contracted Hank to do the deed, is alive and plans with Catherine to bring down Josie.

Unanswered Questions

  • Where is Major Briggs?
  • Where has Andrew Packard been all this time?
