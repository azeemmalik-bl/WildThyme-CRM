12 - Let's Rock

Written by Mark Frost and David Lynch

Directed by David Lynch

Original Airdate: 30 July 2017

Next →
  • Gordon and Albert recruit Tammy and deputise Diane to the Blue Rose task force.
  • Sarah Palmer has an unsettling experience in a grocery store, which leads Deputy Chief Hawk to check on her at her house, where he hears an unxplained noise behind Sarah.
  • Albert interrupts Gordon and his French lady friend to show him a text message Diane received saying "Las Vegas?". Her response is "THEY HAVEN'T ASKED YET".
  • Frank visits Ben Horne to tell him his grandson Richard killed the boy in the hit-and-run, and attempted to kill the only witness - Miriam Sullivan. Ben agrees to pay her medical costs and gives Cooper's old room key to Frank as a memento for Harry.
  • Chantal and Hutch assassinate Warden Murphy in front of his son.
  • Audrey Horne demands that her accountant husband, Charlie, help her find Billy, her missing lover. He reluctantly phones "Tina", and is astonished by what she tells him, but does not tell Audrey what "Tina" said.
  • Diane discovers that the map coordinates on Ruth Davenport's arm point to Twin Peaks.
