6 - Don't Die

Written by Mark Frost and David Lynch

Directed by David Lynch

Original Airdate: 11 June 2017
     
 

Police take Cooper to Dougie's home and Janey-E receives a photo of Dougie with Jade from criminals demanding Dougie's gambling debts plus exorbitant interest.

Janey-E faces down the criminals who accept a smaller sum.

Cooper has another vision of MIKE urging him to "wake up".

Cooper draws cryptic images on the case files, guided by lights on the pages. Bushnell recognises a pattern in the drawings and thanks Cooper.

Duncan Todd sends an envelope marked with a black spot to hitman Ike "The Spike" Stadtler, ordering him to kill Lorraine and Dougie. Ike kills Lorraine and her co-workers, destroying his signature weapon in the process.

Albert finds Diane, Cooper's erstwhile assistant, in a bar.

In Twin Peaks, Red, revealed as a drug supplier, charges Richard Horne with a delivery. Richard speeds in his truck, running over and killing a young boy. Carl Rodd comforts the boy's mother.

Hawk drops a coin in the restroom and finds pages torn from Laura's diary hidden inside a stall door - the hinge of which is marked with a Native American head.
