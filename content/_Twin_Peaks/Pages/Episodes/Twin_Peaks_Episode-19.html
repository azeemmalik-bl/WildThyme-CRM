19 - The Black Widow

Written by Harley Peyton and Robert Engels

Directed by Caleb Deschanel

Original Airdate: 12 January 1991

“ Is a dog man's best friend? I had a dog. The dog was large. It ate my garden, all the plants, and much earth. The dog ate so much earth it died. Its body went back to the earth. I have a memory of this dog. The memory is all that I have left of my dog. He was black – and white. ”

  • Ben Horne continues to slip deeper into insanity while Bobby tries to win his admiration by tailing Hank Jennings and photographs him in a drug dealing meeting.
  • Cooper looks into real estate to buy in thought of staying in Twin Peaks and uncovers recent drug activity at a run-down house called Dead Dog Farm.
  • Truman investigates when Dougie Milford dies from a heart attack on his wedding night, in which Mayor Milford insists that Lana Budding be charged with murder through sex.
  • Nadine hears the call of organized sports and continues to try to win Mike Nelson's affections by body-slamming him while trying out for the school's wrestling team.
  • At the Marsh estate, James learns more about Evelyn's troubled marriage from her chauffeur, (her brother Malcolm), but cannot resist temptation from the femme fatale.
  • Elsewhere, Dick Tremayne takes Little Nicky out camping where the child's attitude scares him after learning from Nicky's case worker that his parents died under mysterious circumstances.
  • Cooper and Truman receive a visit from a visiting Air Force colonel investigating Major Briggs' disappearance who tells them about the Major's top-secret search for the so-called White Lodge.
  • Catherine and Pete Martell continue to gloat and taunt Josie who now works for them as a maid while they attempt a romantic candlelight dinner.
  • Audrey helps Cooper prove his innocence of the drug dealing charges by showing him the photos Bobby took of Ernie Niles, Jean Renault, Hank Jennings and the corrupt Mountie King. Cooper gives the photos to Agent Bryson who arranges a sting at the Dead Dog Farm.
  • Major Briggs reappears to his wife and Bobby in his house.

Unanswered Questions

  • Is Little Nicky the child of the Devil?
  • Where has Major Briggs been while abducted?
