4 - The One-Armed Man

Written by Robert Engels

Directed by Tim Hunter

Original Airdate: 3 May 1990

Next →

“Even the ones who laugh are sometimes caught without an answer: these creatures who introduce themselves but we swear we have met them somewhere before.

Yes, look in the mirror. What do you see? Is it a dream, or a nightmare? Are we being introduced against our will? Are they mirrors?

I can see the smoke. I can smell the fire. The battle is drawing nigh.”

  • Cooper and Truman track down the One-Armed Man who claims not to be called MIKE, and never to have met BOB.
  • Josie Packard was watching Catherine and Benjamin at the motel.
  • Ben and Catherine are planning to burn down the sawmill.
  • Norma Jennings attends a parole board hearing for her husband, Hank, where it appears he will be released. He also appears to have a connection to Josie Packard.
  • Shelly gives Leo's bloodied shirt to Bobby who plants it in Jacques Renault's flat.
  • Audrey, in an attempt to impress Cooper, has begun her own investigation into Laura's death, and has recruited Donna Hayward to help her.

Unanswered Questions

  • What is the connection with Horne's Department Store?
  • Why doesn't the One-Armed Man remember BOB?
  • What is the significance of Hank's domino and the drawing of it he sent to Josie?
