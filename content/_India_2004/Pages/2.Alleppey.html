Alleppey

A splendid train journey north and we arrive in Alleppey (Alappuzha), a pleasant market town surrounded by coconut plantations and built on the canals that service the coir industry of the backwaters.  We find the usual mix of stalls selling fruit, vegetables, pots and pans and even a coffin shop plus the obligatory mounds of rubbish being picked at by crows. !

 
 
We also stopped at Mount Carmel Roman Catholic Latin Cathedral with its black and white graveyard and were able to watch a wedding take place and also climb the tower to get a view over the whole town.  Later on we visited a weaving business and walked to the beach where we met a host of children who were pouring out of their school as it was the end of the school day. Several of them dragged us to the boats where their fathers worked as fishermen.  Happy and welcoming people!
