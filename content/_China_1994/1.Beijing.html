Beijing

.

After a 12-hour flight we arrived into Beijing, capital of China. Originally called Peking, it still has the 3 letter airport code 'PEK'. It felt very exotic but wonderful as we watched a little old lady being helped down the airplane steps.  

 

After checking-in at our hotel, we walked into Tiananman Square for our first flavour of China. We saw some carved marble obelisks and the outside of the Forbidden City by moonlight.
