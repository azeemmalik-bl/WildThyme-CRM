Xi'an

.

We were greeted the next morning with torrential rain for our flight up to Xi'an.

Luckily the weather had improved by the time we arrived there and checked into our newly-built and rather brash hotel.

A rickshaw ride into downtown Xi'an found us wandering through the back streets where all sorts of stalls were selling a whole range of items - from food to drink and from rattan baskets to spices. Baking hot but a very relaxed atmosphere and the local people appeared very friendly.

 
 
 
The Great Mosque in Xi'an is one of the largest in China. The present buildings only date back to the middle of the 18th century, though the mosque might have been established several hundred years earlier. It has been built in a Chinese architectural style with much of the grounds taken up by gardens.
