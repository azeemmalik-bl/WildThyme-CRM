25 - On The Wings Of Love

Written by Harley Peyton and Robert Engels

Directed by Duwayne Dunham

Original Airdate: 4 April 1991

“ The beautiful thing about treasure is that it exists. It exists to be found. How beautiful it is to find treasure. Where is the treasure that, when found, leaves one eternally happy? I think we all know it exists. Some say it is inside us – inside us one and all. That would be strange. It would be so near. Then why is it so hard to find, and so difficult to attain? ”

  • Truman awakens in the murderous embrace of Ms. Jones but survives the attempt on his life and returns to his job. Truman is mystified as to why Thomas Eckhardt should want him dead.
  • A bonsai tree has been delivered to the Sheriff's office. 'Doc' Hayward advises that Windom Earle had been at his house and left his next chess move with Donna.
  • Gordon Cole returns to Twin Peaks to reinstate Cooper into the FBI and to bring the classified file on Windom Earle, who is now eavesdropping on the police station with a microphone which he has hidden in the bonsai tree.
  • Donna Hayward enlists Audrey Horne's help in investigating Donna's mother who may have a past connection with Ben Horne. They witness a strange meeting between the two.
  • When Cole flirts with Shelly at the Double-R Diner, her seductive appearance somehow makes him hear her voice. Enlightened that he can hear (her voice at least) Cole asks Shelly out on a date. Shelly is somewhat embarrassed but, flattered at the attention, accepts.
  • Truman notes that Cooper and Annie Blackburne seem attracted to each other.
  • When Annie sees the symbols that Cooper drew from descriptions from both Major Briggs and the Log Lady she tells them they look similar to markings in Owl Cave.
  • Donna receives a postcard from the departed James Hurley in San Francisco on his way to Mexico, as well as an invitation to join the annual Miss Twin Peaks Beauty Pageant.
  • Audrey continues pursuing Wheeler, but is thwarted by her father who sends her to Seattle on Pine Weasel ecology business.
  • Windom Earle makes contact (in disguise as "Edward Perkins") with Audrey in the local library and reveals that the poem sent by Earle is by Shelley.
  • Agent Cooper and the Sheriff's Department make a midnight trip to the cave where they discover mysterious petroglyph markings and are attacked by an owl. Andy retaliates and embeds his pick-axe in the rock face which exposes a further strange symbol.
  • Over drinks at the Great Northern Hotel Cooper offers a hand of friendship to Annie Blackburne.
  • Windom Earle makes his own way to Owl Cave and inverts the owl symbol, causing the rocks to shake alarmingly.

Unanswered Questions

  • What relevance do the petroglyph markings in Owl Cave have to the events in Twin Peaks?
  • By inverting the owl symbol, what has Windom Earle unleashed?
