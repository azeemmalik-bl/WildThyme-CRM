Vietnam - Ho Chi Minh City
     
 

Some gorgeous tourists (us) by a statue of Ho Chi Minh, Saigon Opera House, a Virgin Mary statue at the rather dull replica of Notre Dame Cathedral with a weird pink (?) pigeon, Saigon Central Post Office (designed by Gustave Eiffel) and a pair of brides to be - one smiling, one grumpy!

Saigon has completely transformed since the end of the Vietnam War. No more so than the US Embassy where, on 29 April 1975, the last few American evacuees were hustled up a ladder on the roof of the Embassy to a waiting helicopter while US Marines cruelly kicked off any desperate South Vietnamese citizens who were also trying to flee.

I remember seeing these tragic scenes on TV when I was a teenager. Ironically the Yankie Dollar has returned with a vengeance: the new blue building in my photo is the turbo-expensive Hyatt Regency Hotel.
