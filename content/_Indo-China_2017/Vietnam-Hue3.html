Vietnam - Huê

Can't help feeling that there is another 'Carry On' film in the making here at the Huê Citadel (Carry On Up the Imperial Enclosure?). Riotously costumed but badly drilled guards slouch around picking their noses, scratching their arses etc. while local Vietnamese tourists try to get themselves snapped in the midst of the action. It's like a mid-70s production of Chu Chin Chow...

Plus abandoned war machinery which is seen everywhere in Vietnam - a legacy of its troubled past.
