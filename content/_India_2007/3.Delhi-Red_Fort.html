Delhi - Red Fort
   
 
 

.

 

The sandstone walls of the Red Fort extend for 2km and vary in height from 18m on the river side to 33m on the city side. Shah Jahan began construction in 1638 and the building was completed in 1648. He never completely moved his capital from Agra to the new city of 'Shahjahanabad' in Delhi because he was deposed and imprisoned in Agra Fort by his son Aurangzeb.

 
     

The Red Fort dates from the very peak of Mughal Power but the Mughal reign from Delhi was a short one: Aurangzeb was the first and last great Mughal emperor to rule from here.  The Yamuna River used to flow right by the eastern edge of the fort, and once filled the 10m-deep moat.

Security was on red-alert due to the tensions between India and Pakistan. We had to pass through barriers manned by armed guards - as they genuinely feared an attack by terrorists which would wreck the tourism industry.
