On Board Ship
     
 
 

This ship also offered top-notch entertainment from a rock'n'roll show (sadly no photos) to an operatic and show tunes night with an excellent sound system and singers who weren't half bad ...
We also had a cooking demonstration from the ship's head chef in an amusing double act with the Maitre'D from the dining room.  This was followed by a tour of the ship's kitchens.
 
The final night's entertainment was due to be Ann Widdecombe but we were spared her dubious talents as she was standing for election to the European Parliament (she got in ...) and she was replaced by Gareth Gates who was excellent, very musical and decidedly charming.
 
Then sailing back across the North Sea to Southampton where we could spot oil rigs in the far distance.
