Vietnam - Hoi An
     
 

Classic views of this graceful and historic town. Once a major port, it has benefitted from the Thu Bon River silting up in the late 19th century so ships could no longer access the town's docks and therefore retaining its easygoing provincial demeanour and harmonious old-town character. It was also virtually untouched during the American War thanks to the co-operation of both sides. It
