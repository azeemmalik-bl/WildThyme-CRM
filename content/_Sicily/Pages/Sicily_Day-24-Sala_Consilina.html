Day 24 - 18 May 2018 (Sala Consilina)

It was a sad farewell to Diane and Stephen this morning after a fabulous week with them. Then, after a challenging 7 1/2 hour drive up through Sicily, across on the ferry, up mountainous passes etc, we arrived in Sala Consilina (a small town clinging to the hillsides) in the middle of a downpour of biblical proportions. Our car got stuck in the narrowest of cobbled streets but, when we arrived at our accommodation, we found we were in an old 18th century Palazzo. Result !!
