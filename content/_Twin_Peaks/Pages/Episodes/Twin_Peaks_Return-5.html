5 - Case Files

Written by Mark Frost and David Lynch

Directed by David Lynch

Original Airdate: 4 June 2017

  • Dougie's would-be assassins call their terrified boss, Lorraine, who sends a message ("Argent 2") that activates a black box in Buenos Aires.
  • An autopsy finds Dougie's wedding ring in the corpse's stomach.
  • Looking into a mirror, Cooper's doppelgänger sees that BOB is still within him.
  • At Dougie's workplace, Cooper reacts to a statue, coffee, and the words "agent" and "case files". A light above his colleague Anthony Sinclair spurs Cooper to accuse him of lying, angering Sinclair and their boss, Bushnell Mullins.
  • The casino's owners, the Mitchum brothers, beat and fire Supervisor Burns, believing his complicity in Cooper's winning streak. Warrick is appointed in his place.
  • Car thieves trigger the bomb under Dougie's car.
  • Jade drops Cooper's Great Northern Hotel room key in a mailbox.
  • Becky and her jobless husband, Steven Burnett, snort drugs after borrowing money from Becky's mother, Shelly.
  • Dr. Jacoby broadcasts conspiracy theories (under his pseudonym "Dr. Amp"), watched by Jerry Horne and Nadine Hurley.
  • At the Pentagon, Colonel Davis learns that another set of Major Briggs' fingerprints (the sixteenth in 25 years) have been found in South Dakota. Lieutenant Cynthia ("Cindy") Knox is despatched to investigate.
  • At the Roadhouse, Richard Horne bribes Deputy Chad Broxford and molests a woman.
  • Tammy discovers Cooper's on-file fingerprints are the reverse of the doppelgänger's.
  • Cooper's doppelgänger frightens Warden Murphy by mentioning a "Mr. Strawberry" and uses his prison phone call to disrupt the security system.
  • The black box becomes a silver ball.
