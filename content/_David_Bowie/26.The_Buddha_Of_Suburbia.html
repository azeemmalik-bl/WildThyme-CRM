1993 - THE BUDDHA OF SUBURBIA
   
 

Originally released in the UK in 1993, The Buddha of Suburbia soundtrack album featured a montage of characters from the TV adaption of Hanif Kureishi's novel of the same name. When the soundtrack was issued in the US in 1995, however, it was given a new design featuring a 1.Outside era photo of Bowie, taken by photographer Frank Ockenfels Ill. The stark, dreamlike shot was a fitting replacement for one of Bowie's more ambient-sounding albums, and has since officially taken its place among the David Bowie album covers.

Photographer: Frank Ockenfels III
