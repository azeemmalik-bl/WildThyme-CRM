Background
 

The Life and Death of King John, a history play is believed to have been written in the mid-1590s but was not published until it appeared in the First Folio in 1623. King John is closely related to an anonymous history play, The Troublesome Reign of King John (c. 1589), the "masterly construction" but infelicitous expression of which led Peter Alexander to argue that Shakespeare's was the earlier play. Shakespeare derived from Holinshed's Chronicles certain verbal collocations and points of action.

 
Plot Summary
 

The play dramatises the reign of John, King of England (ruled 1199-1216), son of Henry II of England and Eleanor of Aquitaine, and father of the future Henry III of England.
