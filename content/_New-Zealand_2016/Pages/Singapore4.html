Singapore
     
 

A return visit to the Tanglin Club where I went as a child with my parents. It was where I learned to swim (I fell in at the deep end and just started paddling). Much of it had changed and not all for the better: it had lost its olde-worlde charm but it was very nice to be welcomed back as an 'honoured guest'. Mister Ram the doorman (who remembered every members' name - even the children) had sadly passed on, but it was still wonderful to come back after 48 years! We also had afternoon tea in the Club Lounge with Yvonne's god-daughter Kimberley.
