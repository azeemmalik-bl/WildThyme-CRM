PLOT

A Scottish lord becomes convinced by a trio of witches that he will become the next King of Scotland, and his ambitious wife supports him in his plans of seizing power.

BACKGROUND

It was announced in March 2019 that Joel Coen, in a rare solo effort, was set to write and direct a new take on the William Shakespeare play. Filming began in Los Angeles on 7 February 2020. To give the film a look "untethered from reality", it was shot entirely on sound stages. The film was shot in black-and-white.

It was announced on 26 March 2020, that filming had been halted due to the COVID-19 pandemic. Production resumed on 23 July 2020 and concluded on 31 July 2020.

The film's score was composed by Carter Burwell, the Coen brothers' longtime collaborator. Regarding Joel directing alone without Ethan, Burwell stated, "Ethan didn't want to make movies anymore. He seems to be happy with what he's just doing. They both have tons of unproduced scripts sitting on shelves, but I don't know what Joel will do without him."
