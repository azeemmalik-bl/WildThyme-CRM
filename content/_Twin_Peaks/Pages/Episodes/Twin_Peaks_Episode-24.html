24 - Wounds And Scars

Written by Barry Pullman

Directed by James Foley

Original Airdate: 28 March 1991
       

" Sometimes - well, let's say all times - things are changing. We are judged as human beings on how we treat our fellow human beings. How do you treat your fellow human beings? At night, just before sleep, as you lay by yourself in the dark, how do you feel about yourself? Are you proud of your behaviour? Are you ashamed of your behaviour? You know in your heart if you have hurt someone you know. If you have hurt someone, don't wait another day before making things right. The world could break apart with sadness in the meantime."

 

• Truman is suffering from grief in the wake of Josie's unexplained death, and confines himself to the Bookhouse, drinking heavily.

• Norma's younger sister, Annie Blackburne, arrives in town and reveals that she has left the convent where she was living for the past five years since age 17, and claims that she is ready to face the world.

• Windom Earle reads in the newspaper of Cooper's latest chess moves, and becomes angry after immediately figuring out that Cooper is getting help.

• Meanwhile, Ben Horne plans to put on a charity event fashion show at the Great Northern in support of the endangered Pine Weasel, with financing from John Justice Wheeler and Dick Tremayne as the MC, who disdains help from Tim Pinkle.

• In the wake of Thomas Eckhardt's murder, his sinister Executive Assistant, Ms. Jones, puts his final plan of revenge into motion by delivering a mysterious box to Catherine.

• In disguise, Windom Earle individually contacts Donna (as "Dr. Gerald Craig") and Shelly (as a leather clad biker).

• Major Briggs and the Log Lady discover that they have similar mysterious markings on their bodies and alert Cooper to this fact. Both "disappeared" in the past and both have recollections of a flash of light and of owls screeching.

• Audrey continues flirting with Wheeler, while Andy tries to get back together with Lucy.

• Ed, through the intervention of Dr. Jacoby, breaks up with Nadine.

• Donna sees Ben Horne arrive at her house and kiss her mother in more than a platonic fashion.

• Norma encourages Shelly to enter the Miss Twin Peaks pageant.

• Cooper finds Norma's sister, Annie, very attractive but also notes the scar on her wrist.

• Cooper is forced to take over as temporary sheriff of Twin Peaks, and has Deputy Hawk help him follow more clues to Windom Earle's whereabouts.

• Mike Nelson and Nadine finally decide to consummate their growing romance by checking into the Great Northern Hotel as a married couple, although Mike is spotted by a school classmate.

• The fashion show descends into disaster when the Pine Weasel escapes and causes panic.

• Ms. Jones slips into bed with the comatose Sheriff Truman.

Unanswered Questions

• What does Ms. Jones hope to achieve with her seduction of Sheriff Truman?

• What are Windom Earle's plans with his three female targets?
