Mercado 20 de Noviembre
Returning to Oaxaca from our morning at Monte Albán, we had a walk about town and made our way to the covered markets.
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
These markets sell everything from decorated skulls, woven baskets, devil masks, painted tin hearts, plastic buckets, paper decorations to hamsters and iguanas.
  
 
 
 
The other side of the street holds the food market selling breads, fruit, cheese, vegetables but the biggest treat for carnivores is the Pasillo de Carnes Asadas (Grilled Meat Passage) where various stalls grill slices of beef and chilli-coated pork over hot coals. The air is thick with smoke and the smell of burning flesh. It was like Swelter’s kitchen in Gormenghast ...
