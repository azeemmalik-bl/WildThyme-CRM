16 - No Knock, No Doorbell

Written by Mark Frost and David Lynch

Directed by David Lynch

Original Airdate: 27 August 2017
     
 

Cooper's doppelgänger and Richard Horne follow two of three coordinates the doppelgänger obtained. Observed by a stoned Jerry Horne, the doppelgänger sends Richard ahead to the site, where he is electrocuted and vaporised.

The doppelganger says "Goodbye, my son" then types out a text reading, ": - ) ALL" and gets in his truck.

While Cooper lies in a coma, both the FBI and Chantal and Hutch are waiting outside the Jones residence. Chantal and Hutch are killed by a Polish neighbour they provoke in a parking dispute.

Cooper awakes from his coma, finally mentally and physically restored to his former self. MIKE tells him that the doppelgänger did not return to the Black Lodge is still at large and gives him the Owl Cave ring. Cooper gives MIKE some of his hair, asking him to make another tulpa, then has the Mitchums organise a flight to Spokane, Washington.

Diane smokes at the hotel bar and receives the text sent by Cooper's doppelgänger. Unnerved, she "remembers" and responds with a series of numbers. She checks her handbag, revealing a gun before walking away from the bar and goes to Gordon, Albert, and Tammy and tells them about the night Cooper's doppelgänger visited her years before.

She says that Cooper raped her and took her to an old gas station. Exclaiming "I'm in the sheriff's station ... I'm not me", she pulls a gun but is shot by Albert and Tammy.

Diane appears in the red room, where MIKE tells her she was manufactured. She bluntly acknowledges this before cursing him out. Her face breaks open, releasing black smoke and a seed before she disintegrates.

Tammy realizes that the "Diane" they had been associating with is actuality a tulpa.

At the Roadhouse, Audrey finally arrives with Charlie and dances to "Audrey's Dance". When a fight breaks out, Audrey begs Charlie to take her home. Suddenly, she finds herself looking in a mirror in a sparse white room, confused.

The Roadhouse band continues to play, but in reverse.
