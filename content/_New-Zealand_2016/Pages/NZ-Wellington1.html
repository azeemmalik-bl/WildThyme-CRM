Wellington

We took a 20 minute bus ride out to Weta Cave: a mini museum of the Academy Award-winning company that brought The Lord of the Rings, King Kong, The Adventures of Tin-Tin and The Hobbit to life.

After that we waited in line to go on one of Wellington's most famous attractions - the little red cable car that clanks up the steep slope from Lambton Quay to Kelburn. The light show in the tunnel made me think that we were visiting Studio 54 instead!
