Cambodia - Phnom Penh
     
 

At the Frangipani Royal Sky Bar - in their lovely roof-top pool. Just the place for a delicious swim.

Views over the Royal Palace Museum, which we visited yesterday, where they are landscaping the gardens...

We come all the way to Cambodia, only to find the highlight of the menu is a "Big English Sausage". After three weeks of exotic flavours, we've gone for the bland option today with tofu burger & chips and ginger fried rice with pork. And some really healthy drinks. Not an artificial colour to be seen anywhere ...
