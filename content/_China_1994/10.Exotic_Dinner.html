Exotic Dinner

.

A tour of the local park and then a quick bit of shopping (a superb embroidered silk hanging which originally hung over the door to a house, and some priests' silk hats with little metal figures on them) and we were whisked back to Guilin as the sun began setting.

 

Back in to the town which is a gourmand's paraside - but from a fairly dubious menu. We looked at tanks and cages of all sorts of stuff (much of which is 'off-limits' - certainly to me - pangolin etc) and which was swishing around in filthy bowls.

 

We finally settled on snake and the restaurant owner chose a "five steps" snake - named because 'allegedly', if it bit you, you'd drop dead within "five steps" ...  He chopped its head off with a pair of rusty scissors (see photo) and then drained its blood into a glass of Chinese whisky. Vile ...

 

And then, while our supper was being prepared and cooked, we had to go off to do some shopping: joss sticks, umbrellas, fortunes, Chinese medicines or buns? Whatever it was I bought (possibly toothpaste), I look very pleased ...

And then back to the restaurant where our snake stew awaited us, plus the glass of whisky with snake blood ...
Imagine having to eat something totally unsavoury (if not totally repulsive) in somewhere as hot as a sauna. That was what this whole experience was like.
And there in front of me is the glass of snake blood still waiting to be consumed ...
 
There was an awful lot of standing up and toasting each other with beer and whisky at this meal. Probably to somehow justify the outrageous cost of our exotic meal and the fact we were being fleeced handsomely.
 
Down the hatch ...
After this experience, there was only one place left to go.
