This enclosed, shaded garden in W2 was improved by painting the wall and trellis the same colour (moss green) and by planting shade-tolerant ferns, hellebores, foxgloves, epimediums and aconites.

This dank back yard in SE10 underwent a modest transformation through the addition of a simple pergola, a thick covering of 20mm shingle on the ground to hide the dull tiles and planting of Phyllostachys nigra, Phormium tenax and Robinia pseudoacacia 'Frisia' in pots.
