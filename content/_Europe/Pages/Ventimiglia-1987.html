Ventimiglia - August 1987

A trip to Italy for the wedding of my old school friend, Jonathan Glanz, to Manuela Raimondo whom he met on holiday in the Maldives.

 

Friends Sarah Buttleman, Rob Hume, Jack Jacouvou, Stephen McDougall, Ian Giddons and Jon's sister Lesley and her husband Mike all flew out to attend the wedding.

We stayed in Bordighera, the next town along the coast from Ventimiglia, where the wedding was to take place at the Town Hall.
 
Other friends (Bernadette and David) and Manuela's relatives (including her brother Vittorio) joined us on the morning of the wedding itself - along with Vita (Jon's relation from Paris) and her pal Ado.
 
 
The ceremony itself was surprisingly informal: the Notary, wearing a sash jauntily around his waist, talked us through the details while best man Sarah, 'natta a Ilford', provided the rings. We then moved outside for confetti and the usual photographs.
 
 
 
All of us then walked down to the seafront to a rather wonderful restaurant where the 'wedding breakfast' and speeches took place. It was baking hot so shirt-sleeves were the order of the day.
