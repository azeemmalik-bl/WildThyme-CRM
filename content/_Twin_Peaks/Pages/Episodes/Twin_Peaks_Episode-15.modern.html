15 - Drive With A Dead Girl

Written by Scott Frost

Directed by Caleb Deschanel

Original Airdate: 17 November 1990

Next →

"Food is interesting. For instance, why do we need to eat? Why are we never satisfied with just the right amount of food to maintain good health and proper energy? We always seem to want more and more.

"When eating too much, the proper balance is disturbed and ill health follows. Of course, eating too little food throws the balance off in the opposite direction and there is the ill health coming at us again.

"Balance is the key. Balance is the key to many things. Do we understand balance? The word 'balance' has seven letters. Seven is difficult to balance, but not impossible if we are able to divide. There are, of course, the pros and cons of division."

  • James Hurley and Donna Hayward miss the chance to say goodbye to Maddy.
  • Leland Palmer (under the direction of BOB) hides the dead Maddy Ferguson in his golf bag and goes for a joyride.
  • Leland has now been totally possessed by BOB, and dances convulsively, as well as taking pleasure in the pain of others. All aspects of Leland's character have been over-ridden.
  • Lucy arrives back in Twin Peaks with her visiting, equally simpleton older sister, Gwen Morton, while Andy tells Lucy he wants to get back together with her.
  • With Ben Horne in jail for Laura Palmer's murder, Jerry tries to arrange a defense for him. Cooper doubts Ben's guilt, but Truman thinks otherwise.
  • Bobby decides to blackmail Ben as the recording (which he found in Leo's boots) reveals Ben giving Leo instructions to burn down the sawmill. Since Ben is already in jail, this might be hard.
  • Norma Jennings' estranged mother, Vivian Smythe, brings her new husband Ernie Niles for a visit, while Hank reappears after being gone for several days and reveals to have known Ernie from time in prison. Ernie is posing as a businessman and has probably told many more lies about his past as well.
  • The One-Armed Man escapes from police custody with a warning that BOB is nearby. Cooper's and Truman's search for BOB gets a boost from the One-Armed Man, who is suffering from an identity crisis.
  • Pete Martell later visits Ben in jail with a taunting audio message from Catherine saying that she only will back up his alibi if he surrenders all his property over to her.
  • It may be assumed that everything Leland has done since Maddy's murder is BOB alone. It could also be argued that everything Leland has done since his hair turned white (like BOB's) has been BOB. However, there are several occasions on which Leland is kind, thoughtful and considerate, such as when he comforted Maddy before his arrest. It is hard to imagine BOB behaving like this, even if it was a disguise. It can be assumed that BOB intended to kill Cooper with the golf club. MIKE's re-capture was all that saved him.
  • Maddy Ferguson's body has been found.
