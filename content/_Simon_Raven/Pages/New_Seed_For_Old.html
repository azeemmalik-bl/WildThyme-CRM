Plot Summary

Lord Canteloupe wants a satisfactory heir so that his dynasty may continue. Unfortunately he is impotent and his existing heir, little Tully Sarum. is not of sound mind.

His wife Theodosia is prepared to do her duty when a suitable partner is found. However, finding the man and the occasion proves somewhat tricky and it is not until Lord Canteloupe goes up to Lord's for the first match of the season that progress is made.
