28 - Miss Twin Peaks

Written by Barry Pullman

Directed by Tim Hunter

Original Airdate: 10 June 1991
       

(Holding log directly in front of her, instead of the usual position cradled in her arms.)

" A log is a portion of a tree.

(Turning end of log to camera.)

" At the end of a crosscut log - many of you know this - there are rings. Each ring represents one year in the life of the tree. How long it takes to a grow a tree! I don't mind telling you some things. Many things I mustn't say. Just notice that my fireplace is boarded up.

(She glances over her right shoulder at the fireplace and the camera moves over to it, where we see the opening covered by plywood.)

" There will never be a fire there.

(Camera pans up to the mantel where we see a vase, a pipe stand, and some old pictures - none recognizable.)

" On the mantelpiece, in that jar, are some of the ashes of my husband.

(Camera pans back down to Log Lady.)

" My log hears things I cannot hear. But my log tells me about the sounds, about the new words. Even though it has stopped growing larger, my log is aware."

 

• Windom Earle has handcuffed both Leo and Major Briggs. Leo manages to release the Major with a plea to "save Shelly". A hideously ashen Windom Earle discovers and retaliates by tying Leo to a lethal device and leaves both the cabin and Leo to an uncertain fate.

• Norma looks forward to the Miss Twin Peaks Beauty Pageant as a way to move forward after Laura Palmer's death and it is revealed that Norma was a previous winner of the Pageant.

• Ben continues to seek a path to goodness, while Audrey confides that she is missing Wheeler but also that her business trip to Seattle has revealed that the Twin Peaks Savings & Loan is supporting Catherine's development plans for Ghostwood.

• Cooper confides to Truman that, at the time of Josie's death she was "quaking like an animal" and, as she died, Cooper saw BOB who seemed to be drawn by her fear. Cooper believes there is a connection between BOB's appearance and the Black Lodge - previously described by Truman as "the evil in these woods". All this is overheard by Windom Earle via the bonsai tree.

• Mr. Pinkle rehearses dance moves with the other contestants while Lana seduces Dick Tremayne to get him to vote for her in the Beauty Pageant.

• Cooper and Annie consummate their relationship after she calls round to his hotel room to ask for help with her Beauty Pageant speech.

• Nadine reveals to Dr. Jacoby that she wants to marry Mike Nelson as soon as Big Ed and Norma are married.

• The delusional and dehydrated Major Briggs is found by Deputy Hawk and returned to the police station where he makes little sense about his captivity, but leaves clues to how to find the Black Lodge.

• Andy discovers that the cave painting is a map to the lodge itself but finds it difficult to pass this information to Cooper.

• Andrew and Pete manage to get the last silver box open to reveal a safety deposit key.

• Donna finally learns the secret from Ben Horne about him and her mother - Ben is actually her biological father.

• Lucy finally chooses Andy to be the father for her baby.

• The Beauty Pageant gets underway as Windom Earle arrives at the event disguised as the Log Lady intending to abduct Annie.

Unanswered Questions

• Where will Windom Earle take Annie?

• As Andy realises the cave painting is a map, can he use it to find the Black Lodge entrance?
