This was an uninspiring bare roof. The client wanted an enclosed, private terrace which has been achieved by the clever use of trellis, decking with integral planters and mirror panels behind the trellis to give the appearance of an extended space.
   
 
To create a calm environment, planting is muted, restrained and scented. Bay standards and evergreen scented Trachelospermum climbers give year round structure while Stipa tenuissima, Potentilla fruticosa and yellow and pink climbing roses provide a delicate backdrop to an excellent outdoor space.
 
 
As with any roof terrace, structural integrity and safety is imperative. A solid balustrade was installed behind the trellis to give support and comply with building regulations.
