Ventimiglia - August 1988

The following year, 1988, I returned to Ventimiglia and this time stayed in Manuela's flat which was only five minutes from the beach.

Here we are waiting for some home-made croissants to be cooked for breakfast.

The view from the kitchen window was of other balconies with the ubiquitous green canopies.
 
We took a bus one day up to Dolceacqua - a pretty village up in the hills in the 'Ligurian hinterland'.
 
 
Ventimiglia's beach was just at the end of the road from Manuela's apartment.  It was a pebbly beach but very clean and the water was clear and very safe in which to swim.
 
 
Jon and Manuela came out to Ventimiglia (I think they must have stayed with her parents, Dino and Bruna, as we didn't have to move out of her apartment) and they took us up into the Ligurian hills above the town.
 
Returning into town, it was all too much for Ian who promptly dozed off ...
Another day we took the train to Turin and, as luck would have it, the Turin Shroud was on display. Some claim the image depicts Jesus of Nazareth and the fabric is the burial shroud in which he was wrapped after crucifixion. First mentioned in 1354, the shroud was denounced in 1389 by the local bishop of Troyes as a fake. Currently the Catholic Church neither formally endorses nor rejects the shroud, and in 2013 Pope Francis referred to it as an “icon of a man scourged and crucified”. The shroud has been kept in the royal chapel of the Cathedral of Turin since 1578.
 
 
Our final jaunt away from Ventimiglia took us over the border to Nice in France where we visited the Cemetery at the Monastère de Cimiez and had a fine lunch in the centre of old Nice.
