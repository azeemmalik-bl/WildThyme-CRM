Vietnam - Hanoi

A late night taxi ride into Hanoi from the airport, our hotel room which appears to double as a fish tank and a charming local bar. The next morning we see what appears to be an Eduardo Paolozzi sculpture in a lake: this is in fact a B52 bomber which was downed into this lake (Huu Tiep) in central Hanoi in December 1972.
