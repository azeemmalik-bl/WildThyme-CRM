Sacred Valley and Pisac
 

Our acclimatisation leisure day took in a visit to the hills above Cuzco from where we enjoyed a panoramic view of the city, then onto the Sacred Valley, the Inca site and market town of Pisac and a warm-up hike over part of the Inca Trail.

Then into a small homestead (note model bulls on the roof) where they farmed Guinea Pigs which are apparently a delicacy in Peru.  NB: I never ate one during my time in the country.  Honest ...
