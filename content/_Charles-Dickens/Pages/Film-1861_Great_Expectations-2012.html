FILMING LOCATIONS

The former Gillette headquarters on the A4 into London was used as the main filming location. The interior was transformed into a Victorian era street scene.

Englefield House at Theale near Reading in Berkshire was used as the location for Satis House.

The production also shot many scenes in Kent including St Thomas a Becket Church in Fairfield, Swale Nature Reserve Shellness, Oare and Elmley Marshes, Stangate Creek, The Historic Dockyard in Chatham and Thames and Medway Canal.
