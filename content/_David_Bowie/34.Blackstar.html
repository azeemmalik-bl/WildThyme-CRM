2016 - BLACKSTAR
   
 

Officially stylised as Blackstar, David Bowie's final album, inspired one of his most minimalist album covers: a simple black star, with five fragmented stars beneath, spelling the name "Bowie". "This was a man who was facing his own mortality" designer Jonathan Barnbrook said at the time of the album's release. "The Blackstar symbol, rather than writing 'Blackstar', has a sort of finality, a darkness, a simplicity, which is a representation of the music." It also referenced the glam-rock that Bowie had found fame with in the early '70s, and Bowie's own fascination with space, which ran through his entire career - including "the idea of a black hole sucking everything in, the Big Bang, the start of the universe, if there is an end to the universe".

Designer: Jonathan Barnbrook
