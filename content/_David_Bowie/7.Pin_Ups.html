1973 - PIN UPS
   
 

Most David Bowie album covers seemed more like fashion shoots than record sleeves. Having himself become a pin-up in the early 70s, Bowie posed with 60s supermodel Twiggy (whom he'd sung about as "Twig the wonder kid" on the Aladdin Sane cut Drive-In Saturday) for Vogue magazine, and then decided he'd rather use the shot himself for a covers album that saw him record glammed-up versions of Swinging 60s classics by Pink Floyd (See Emily Play), Them (Here Comes The Night), Pretty Things (Rosalyn) and more.
