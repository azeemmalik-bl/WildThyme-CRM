Udaipur - Jagdish Temple

.

150m north of the City Palace is the fantastically carved Indo-Aryan Jagdish Temple which was built by Maharaja Jagat Singh in 1651.

It enshrines a black stone image of Vishnu as Jagannath, Lord of the Universe.
The temple steps are flanked by stone elephants and there is intricate and ornate carving around all the plinths and outside the temple walls.
