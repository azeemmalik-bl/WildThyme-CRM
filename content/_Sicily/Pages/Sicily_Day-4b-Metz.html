Day 4 - 28 April 2018 (Metz)

Nicknamed the 'Good Lord's Lantern', Metz Cathedral has one of the highest naves and the largest expanse of stained glass in the world (including more by Marc Chagall, whose work I'm perhaps now re-evaluating). Built from the local yellow Jaumont limestone, it was constructed between 1220 and 1520 after the earlier sack of Metz by Attila the Hun in 451.

However, in 1877, it was heavily damaged after a conflagration due to fireworks and the western facade was completely rebuilt - hence its astonishingly perfect appearance.
