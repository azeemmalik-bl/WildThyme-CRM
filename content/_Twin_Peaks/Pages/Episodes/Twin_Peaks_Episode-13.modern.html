13 - Demons

Written by Harley Peyton and Robert Engels

Directed by Lesli Linka Glatter

Original Airdate: 3 November 1990

Next →

"Sometimes we want to hide from ourselves - we do not want to be us - it is too difficult to be us. It is at these times that we turn to drugs or alcohol or behavior to help us forget that we are ourselves.

"This of course is only a temporary solution to a problem which is going to keep returning, and sometimes these temporary solutions are worse for us than the original problem.

"Yes, it is a dilemma. Is there an answer? Of course there is: as a wise person said with a smile: 'The answer is within the question.'"

  • Donna and Maddy find themselves at the mercy of an enraged Harold Smith who has gone mad. Donna and Maddy are rescued by James but are forced to leave Laura's secret diary behind.
  • Cooper informs Ben of his rescue of Audrey from One-Eyed Jack's, and Truman identifies Jean Renault as the mastermind behind the whole thing.
  • Meanwhile, Shelly Johnson and Bobby Briggs hold a "welcome home" party for the catatonic Leo, but are disappointed of the low amount of insurance money they get. They take out their vengeance on the comatose Leo by humiliating him.
  • Jonathan threatens Josie that he will kill Sheriff Truman if she refuses to return to Hong Kong with him.
  • Josie then demands the insurance money for the sawmill from Ben who gives her Tojamura's $5 million check. Josie then leaves with Jonathan, despite desperate pleas from Harry.
  • Later Gordon Cole, Cooper's superior, arrives in Twin Peaks to assist Cooper's investigation, warn Cooper of threats and to help find the mysterious one-armed man who holds a clue to the identity of Laura Palmer's killer.
  • He also warns Cooper of Windom Earle, from whom he delivers a cryptic note regarding a chess move.
  • Big Ed continues to deal with the crazy Nadine and her super-human powers.
  • Maddy says goodbye to James as she prepares to leave Twin Peaks.
  • Interrogating the One-Armed Man, Cooper withholds his medication whereupon MIKE emerges who gives them information about the evil spirit Killer BOB, who inhabits a person that lives in Twin Peaks.
  • MIKE, like BOB, is an "inhabiting spirit", who has been using Gerard's body to track down BOB - who was once his "familiar" - to stop him from killing again. BOB is a spirit who takes over peoples' bodies and uses them to feed. BOB's food is fear and the "pleasures".
  • BOB is now at the Great Northern Hotel.

Unanswered Questions

  • As Harold Smith had Laura's secret diary, could he have killed her?
  • Who is BOB's current host?
  • Why did Windom Earle send Cooper a chess move?
  • What will Harold Smith do now that he is "unclean"?
  • Josie obviously did not want to leave. Why did she have to go?
