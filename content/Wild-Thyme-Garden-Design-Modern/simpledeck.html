Simple Deck, Edgware

This new-build site offered a blank canvas. The result? A smart deck and lush, vibrant planting - easy to maintain and good on the eye.

Planting here is strong and tall - Agapanthus, Syringa, Photinia, Callicarpa, Phormiums and Bamboo.

Ground cover is provided by Heuchera and Festuca glauca.
