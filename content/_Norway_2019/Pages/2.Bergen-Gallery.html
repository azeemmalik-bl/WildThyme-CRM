Bergen - KODE Museum

The Rasmus Meyer Collection in Bergen's KODE Museum features many works by Edvard Munch and highlights from the careers of Norwegian artists including Johan Christian Dahl, Harriet Backer, Erik Werenskiold and Gerhard Munthe.

The building opened in 1924. It was designed by architect Ole Landmark to house the extensive art gift donated in 1916 by businessman and art collector Rasmus Meyer..
