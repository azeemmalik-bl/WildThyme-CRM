Balloon Trip - Egypt 2008
to

A very early start in order to be taken over to the West Bank for an exhilarating 45 minute hot air balloon ride. First of all we could see a pin-prick of light in the sky. As it came nearer we could see that it was a balloon - soon followed by others - all flying on the even earlier morning session.

A quick boat ride (with complimentary breakfast of coffee and madeira cake) over to the west bank and we could see more and more balloons coming in to land. We had to clamber into the basket of the incoming balloon as the previous passengers climbed out - one for one - so that the weight balance was not jeopardised. No safety talk, no life jackets, just "hang on" and we were up and away.

 
 
 

Safety was not paramount on the trip, but I enjoyed a thrilling sense of exhilaration at seeing the ground below (and hearing voices clearly rising up from the ground). In fact, after we returned back to the UK, we heard of a fatal crash when a balloon ploughed into power cables on the ground below. I was glad we didn't know of this when we were flying over the electricity supply to the area ...

Then the same one out / one in routine and we all clambered out and the basket filled up with its next load of passengers and they were off to do the ride all over again.
