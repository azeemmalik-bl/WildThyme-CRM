Ventimiglia - October 2002

Another visit to Ventimiglia - this time in October (I had always been in high Summer in the past) and the weather was noticeably cooler and many of the tourist restaurants had closed for the season.

However, we did have a couple of days when the temperature was still plenty warm enough to sit on the beach and eat out at lunchtime.

Another visit to the Villa Hanbury - and this time we explored the succulent garden, including some marvellous Agaves, Dasylirions and Cycads.
 
 
Another visit to Dolceaqua - this time in the rain.
 
We took a train trip up to Turin and went to Turin Cathedral and also found the magnificent National Museum of Cinema which is housed in the Mole Antonelliana, named after its architect, Alessandro Antonelli. A mole in Italian is a building of monumental proportions. Construction began in 1863, soon after Italian unification, and was completed in 1889, after the architect's death. Originally conceived as a synagogue, it now houses the Cinema Museum, and is believed to be the tallest museum in the world.
