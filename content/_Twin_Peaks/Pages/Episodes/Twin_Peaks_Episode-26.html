26 - Variations On Relations

Written by Mark Frost and Harley Peyton

Directed by Jonathan Sanger

Original Airdate: 11 April 1991

“ Pie. Whoever invented the pie? Here was a great person. In Twin Peaks, we specialise in cherry pie and huckleberry pie. We do have many other types of pies, and at the Double-R Diner, Norma knows how to make them all better than anyone I have ever known. I hope Norma likes me. I know I like her and respect her. I have spit my pitch gum out of my mouth onto her walls and floors and sometimes onto her booths. Sometimes I get angry and do things I'm not proud of. I do love Norma's pies. I love pie *with* coffee.”

  • Cooper and Truman find the large map-like petroglyph discovered by Windom Earle in Owl Cave and try to unlock its secret. They enlist Major Briggs who agrees to help explain Windom Earle's role in Project Blue Book (the UFO investigation).
  • Windom Earle picks up a heavy metal youth with the promise of beer to whom he confides his intentions to find the mysterious Black Lodge.
  • Catherine shows Pete and Truman the mysterious puzzle box left behind by Thomas Eckhardt and, once opened, it reveals another box inside.
  • As the town prepares for the Miss Twin Peaks Beauty Pageant, the conniving Lana Budding Milford enters as a contestant and persuades the elderly and senile Mayor Milford, who is one of the judges, to vote in her favour.
  • Cooper learns about the love poem that Shelly, Donna and Audrey received from Windom Earle, and correctly concludes that Leo is also working for Earle.
  • Dick Tremayne hosts a wine-tasting fund raiser at the Great Northern for Ben Horne's opposition to Catherine's development plans for Ghostwood.
  • Leo begins to show disillusionment to Earle over his methods, but is held in check.
  • Cooper continues dating Annie Blackburne, unaware that Windom Earle knows about their newfound romance.
  • Gordon Cole goes on a date with Shelly and kisses her goodbye as he leaves Twin Peaks but is seen by Bobby.
  • With Audrey out of town, Wheeler confides in Cooper about his feelings for Audrey, before receiving a telegram which causes him to check out of the Great Northern Hotel.
  • Donna continues to investigate her parents’ past which may involve Ben Horne.
  • A large crate is discovered in the town bandstand. When opened by Cooper it is found to contain the body of the heavy metal youth entombed in an elaborate, large, chess pawn.

Unanswered Questions

  • What will the map found in Owl Cave lead Cooper to discover?
  • What will be Windom Earle's next chess move?
