Stockholm and Copenhagen - February 1997

A work trip to Stockholm for meetings with the Drottningholm Opera and Svenska Rikskonserter. Bizarrely, I stayed in a hotel which had been converted from a prison ...

Stockholm city centre was smart and very business-orientated - but very cold!

After my meetings I took a 35 minute flight over to Copenhagen where I met with my pal Ian, who was there for his own work meetings the following day. While he was doing those, I explored Copenhagen town centre which seemed similar in many respects to Stockholm.

The Tivoli Gardens are a world-famous amusement park and pleasure garden. The park opened on 15 August 1843 and is the third-oldest operating amusement park in the world, after Dyrehavsbakken in nearby Klampenborg, also in Denmark, and Wurstelprater in Vienna.

We met for lunch and then saw this marvellous spectacle: children beating the hell out of a container filled with oranges until it broke and all the fruit cascaded out. Similar to the piñata tradition from Mexico, the Danish version is called slå katten af tønden ("hit the cat out of the barrel") in which a wooden barrel is struck to release candy or, in this case, fruit.
 

Our final night in Copenhagen was spent at Det Kongelige Teater (Old Stage) to see Poulenc's Dialogues of the Carmelites.
