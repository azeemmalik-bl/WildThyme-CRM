Cambodia - Phnom Penh to Tonlé Sap

On the road from Phnom Penh to Tonlé Sap Lake we stopped at this market. They sell the 'local delicacy' - deep fried insects: spiders, maggots (silkworm larvae), beetles, cockroaches, crickets and water snakes. All of which seemed absolutely disgusting and I wouldn't try any. In fact, it still makes me shudder when I think about it.
